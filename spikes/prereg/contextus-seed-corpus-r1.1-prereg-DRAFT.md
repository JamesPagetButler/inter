Status: **DRAFT r1, 2026-11-17. Not frozen. Nothing run.** Director: qbp-architecture. Spike: inter#224. Design source: inter#300 (v0.3, stamped by cartan under C1–C5; D8–D15 since).

Grade: **napkin**. No outcome raises any claim above napkin, and nothing is written to Contextus, Wyrd, BMA or CTH.

## 0. Why

Round 1 is no-go on grader agreement (κ 0.494, report §1). The RCA (`run1/rca/`, table v2 sha256 be281357…e22f) attributes 14 of 19 disagreements to the rubric: **H1**, undefined relation vocabulary (8), and **H6**, rubric asymmetry (6). Only 1 is a lean-grader error.

The beekeeper directed, first-hand on 2026-11-17: "I propose that we regrade round 1 work using the output of what you design."

**Round 1.1 tests the judging side alone.** It re-grades round 1's existing claims under the v1 vocabulary and rubric, with the inter#300 grading protocol. The claims, inputs and extractor are unchanged, so any change in agreement comes from judging.

## 1. Questions

- **Q1 (primary):** under the v1 rubric, does verifier-vs-checker agreement on claims that did **not** shape the rules clear the bar, judged on its paper-cluster lower bound?
- **Q2:** is any agreement **valid**, not just shared? Two raters can agree and both be wrong.
- **Q3:** does agreement survive without W7160385925 (6 of 8 H1 items came from that one paper)?
- **Q4:** what does the protocol cost and load? Measured: doubt rate, escalation volume per layer, tokens.

## 2. Inputs (fixed, from round 1)

- **Claims:** the 387 graded claims of round 1 (Haiku 190, Opus 197), 20 papers, keyed by `s5_arm_key_A2_SEALED.json`. Raters are **blind to arm**.
- **Text:** `s5_grader_inputs_A2/` at inter ab6c43a, unchanged: statements only, ≤ ~10k tokens.
- **The contamination split, pre-stated:**
  - **contaminated (62)** = the 50 spot-check claims (`s5_spotcheck_A2`) plus the 12 Gemini-disagreement RCA items. The rules were written while reading these. They are reported separately and **never enter a gate**.
  - **headline pool (325)** = everything else, across all 20 papers (7 to 19 per paper; maths 152, physics 173).
- **Known gaps, by construction:**
  - no `quote` field, so locator-cause items (RCA 5, 12, 17) are **untested** here;
  - no `hypotheses` or `framework` fields;
  - old 9-relation vocabulary, with no `has-property` or `subobject-of`.
  These belong to round 1.2.

## 3. Raters and rubric

- **The rubric is generated from the v1 relation registry** (inter#300 D2: 10 stored types, six-part definitions signed by qbp-oppenheimer, generated by code). Its sha256 is pinned at freeze.
- **C3 parity:** every rater run records the sha of the rubric it received. Any mismatch with the pinned sha **voids the run**.
- **Verifier v1:** a fresh Opus sub-agent per paper (the `corpus-grader` lineage, Read only), dispatched by the director, receiving the generated rubric.
  - Per claim it outputs: the grade (the round-1 categories), a reason quoting the passage, and a **doubt flag** with its reason.
  - After its grades are fixed and hashed, it writes the **P4 close-out report** (friction / doubt / missing context / proposed change / process observation). That report goes to the refinement channel and is never read by another rater.
- **Checker v1 (different family):** Gemini, called directly by script with the same generated rubric (the A2 redo pattern: `generate_content`, request bytes hashed before the call). It grades the **double-graded set** (§4) and the L1 items.
- **One verifier per claim, plus an L1 second opinion on doubt** (beekeeper, 2026-11-17).

## 4. Samples (seeded, drawn and committed before any rating)

The seed is **251117**, a PCG stream, and the sampler script is committed and hashed before the draw.

- **Double-graded set (C1):** **6 claims per paper from the headline pool, 120 in all.** No paper exceeds 5% of the set (cap ≤ 15%). The verifier and the checker grade all 120.
- **Agreed-item audit (C4):** **20 items**, drawn after rating from headline claims where verifier and checker agreed. Two blind labellers grade them: **qbp-oppenheimer** (a seat; allowed in spikes, P5) and a **separate fresh Gemini labeller session** with its own prompt. The Gemini overlap with the checker family is recorded as a limitation. Labeller disagreements go to L2.
- **Calibration split:** the 120 are split 60 / 60 (calibration / held-out) by the same seed, **committed before any grader v2 is written**. Round 1.1 tunes nothing.

## 5. Escalation (the inter#300 P3 ladder; spike staffing per P5)

1. **L0, code:** schema, rubric sha, every grade in the category set.
2. **L1:** any verifier doubt goes to the checker. Two agreeing raters settle it.
3. **L2:** for an L1 split, fresh verifier and checker sessions receive both positions plus the passage and must converge, or produce an impasse record (MO §3 / §10).
4. **L3:** qbp-oppenheimer applies the rubric to L2 impasses. **Budget ≤ 30 items.** Rule-meaning disputes skip L3 (seq 5212).
5. **L4:** panel of qbp-oppenheimer + qbp-architecture, with cartan as confirmer. Alternate: *pending, cth-implementor*.
6. **L5:** the beekeeper, batched.

Every L3 or L4 ruling becomes a calibration item, plus a proposed rubric rule where it exposes ambiguity. **It applies forward only and never re-scores 1.1.**

## 6. Measurements

- **κ, faithful vs not,** verifier v1 vs checker v1, on the 120. Point estimate, plus a **paper-cluster bootstrap 95% CI** (B = 10000, seed 251117).
- **The same κ without W7160385925** (114 claims, 19 papers).
- **The audit:** the share of the 20 where both labellers agree with the agreed grade.
- **Precision under v1** (verifier, all 325 headline claims): per arm, per domain, Wilson 95%. Plus the per-category distribution against round 1.
- **The contaminated 62,** reported separately and labelled circular.
- **Load:** doubt rate (planning figure 15%), items reaching each layer, L3 against its budget, tokens per stage (four-field), and P4 reports clustered by RCA cause.

## 7. Predictions (pre-stated, directional, not gates)

- **P1:** pooled Haiku precision under v1 (headline) is **below 0.889** (round 1). The cause: R-proves rejects "object proves P".
- **P2:** W7160385925's Haiku precision **drops by more than the pooled drop.**

If neither holds, either raters aren't applying R-proves or the RCA reading was wrong. That goes to L4 as a rubric-application review. It is not a gate failure.

## 8. Decision rules and consequence table (exhaustive; the round-1 §8 gap must not recur)

Evaluated in this order: validity, then G1, then G2, then G3.

- **Validity:** a C3 rubric-sha mismatch, a §2a safety stop or a §10 kill makes the run **void**: recorded, no outcome read.
- **G1 (primary):** κ cluster-bootstrap **lower bound ≥ 0.6** on the 120.
- **G2 (validity):** the audit passes if **≥ 17 of 20** agreed items are confirmed by both labellers.
- **G3 (concentration):** the κ lower bound without W7160385925 is ≥ 0.6.

| Outcome | Consequence | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Void | Recorded with its cause. One re-run is allowed only under a signed amendment that names the cause's fix. | qbp-architecture |
| G1 pass, G2 pass, G3 pass | **The judging side is validated for 1.2.** Rubric v1 and verifier v1 freeze as 1.2's judging baseline. The calibration split stands. | qbp-architecture |
| G1 pass, G2 pass, G3 fail | **It passes only on one paper's pattern.** 1.2 proceeds, but its double-graded set must test H1 breadth as a primary measure. No claim that v1 generalises. | qbp-architecture |
| G1 pass, G2 fail | **Agreement without validity.** Rubric/verifier revision goes through L4 before 1.2 is pre-registered. Recorded as a negative on validity. | L4 panel |
| G1 fail, point κ ≥ 0.6 | **Inconclusive (underpowered).** Not claimed validated. 1.2's double-graded set is sized from 1.1's measured variance. | qbp-architecture; deming |
| G1 fail, point κ < 0.6 | **The RCA hypothesis is not supported** ("vocabulary drove κ"). L4 reviews. No 1.2 pre-reg until the rubric is revised. | L4 panel |
| Any outcome not listed above | **The director chooses no consequence.** The record goes to the L4 panel, then the beekeeper (L5). | L4 → beekeeper |

**Every outcome:**
- round 1's record is unchanged;
- 1.1's grades are additive, in `run1/s5_grades_r1.1_v1/`, never over `s5_grades_A2_lean/`;
- the P4 clusters and L3/L4 rulings feed the refinement queue;
- the report states the known gaps (§2).

### 8b. Repeatability probe (beekeeper, 2026-11-17: "pick 1 paper and run it 5 times to see how much variance we see")

This is a small **paid** step. It runs **after** the §8a code tests pass and **before** the 1.1 run. Reliability caps validity: if a rater disagrees with itself, cross-rater κ can't be read beyond that.

- **Paper:** drawn by seed 251117 from the 20 papers, **excluding W7160385925** (its pattern is tested by G3). The draw is committed before any run.
- **Runs:** the verifier v1 (a fresh Opus sub-agent each time) **and** the checker v1 (Gemini, fresh session each time), **5 runs each** on every graded claim of that paper. The inputs and rubric sha are identical. Request bytes are hashed per run.
- **Measured:**
  - per claim, the number of distinct grades across the 5 runs;
  - the **unanimous-claim share**;
  - **Fleiss' κ across the 5 runs** (self-agreement), per rater;
  - doubt-flag stability;
  - per-run token spread;
  - whether the 5 P4 reports name the same friction.
- **Pre-stated design rule** (fixed now, so the probe can't tune the design after the fact). If a rater's 5-run self-agreement κ is **below 0.8**:
  - that rater's 1.1 grades become the **majority of 3 independent runs** per claim (ties go to L1);
  - G1 is evaluated on those majority grades;
  - deming re-costs the change before the run. It roughly triples that rater's spend.
  Otherwise single runs stand.
- **Isolation:** the probe's outputs are **not** 1.1 grades. They are committed under `run1/s5_r1.1_probe/`, and nothing in the rubric or gates changes from them, except through the pre-stated rule above.
- **Cost (provisional):** ≈ 0.25M Opus (5 × ~49k) + ≈ 0.1–0.15M Gemini, inside the 1.1 grant.

The round 1.2 components get the same treatment in their own pre-reg, before any 1.2 run: the LaTeX/ar5iv parser, the location-aware `ltx_ERROR` rule (the 1904.09966 / hep-th/9606001 / footnote-only cases), the chunk-plan validator, the coverage record, support-map seeding, and the route-grade function (MAX over routes, MIN within a route).

## 10. Resources and kill conditions (deming, live-test seq 5269; four-field unit)

**Measured bases:** Opus lean grading ≈ 48.6k per paper. A Gemini call is ≈ 20k, mostly paper text.

| Item | Base estimate |
|---|---|
| Verifier v1, 20 papers (≈ 50–60k each, including the rubric, doubt flags and P4) | 1.0–1.2M Opus |
| §8b probe: verifier ×5 | 0.25–0.3M Opus |
| Checker v1 on the 120 (one call per paper) | ≈ 0.45M Gemini |
| **L1 on doubt (≈ 15% of 387): full paper text, batched per paper** | ≈ 0.4M Gemini |
| Audit labeller (20 items) | ≈ 0.08M Gemini |
| §8b probe: checker ×5 | 0.1–0.15M Gemini |
| **Base grant** | **≈ 1.5M Opus + ≈ 1.1M Gemini**, each with the 1.5× kill line |

- **Why L1 gets full text (director's choice):** every rater sees the **same input form**. A passage-only second opinion would reintroduce H7, input mismatch. The extra ≈ 0.17M is the price of comparability.
- **Majority-of-3 (only if §8b triggers it):**
  - verifier: ≈ 3.0–3.6M Opus;
  - checker: ≈ +0.9M Gemini.
  This is **not pre-granted.** It is a separate **pre-stated top-up request** with the beekeeper's own `--signoff`, filed only if the probe triggers it. The probe runs first, inside the base grant.
- **Per-verifier stop: 65k (A3).** A 60k mean is tight on dense papers, so the **probe's per-run actuals must confirm the stop** before the 20-paper run. If any probe run exceeds 65k, the stop is re-set by signed amendment **before** the run, not after.
- **Box:** API-bound. Scripts run under `boxrun`, 1 core, BLAS pinned. Disk ≤ 50 MB.
- **Kill conditions:**
  - tokens > 1.5× the grant;
  - a **safety decline** halts at that paper, never rerouted (§2a);
  - wall clock > 8 h;
  - the box-pause file is present.
- **Staggered with 1.2,** never concurrent.
