# Restatement prompt (frozen with the round 1 pre-registration)

You will receive text taken from one mathematics or physics paper: its abstract, its introduction, and the statements of its theorems, definitions, propositions, lemmas and conjectures. Each statement is preceded by its label and number as printed (for example "Theorem 3.2").

Rewrite this material as a plain, neutral restatement:
- Keep every labelled statement, with its label and number exactly as printed, and its section number where given.
- Keep every citation marker that appears inside a statement or next to it (for example "[12]" or "(Smith 2019)").
- Keep the mathematical content exact. Do not simplify, generalise, or "correct" any statement.
- Remove rhetorical framing (motivation, praise of prior work, outlook) unless it states a mathematical relation.
- Do not add anything that is not in the text: no interpretation, no connections to other fields, no commentary.

Output only the restatement, as Markdown.
