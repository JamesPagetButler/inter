## 1. Introduction

The paper addresses the Dirac operator for the standard Podleś quantum sphere [14]. It seeks a Dirac operator satisfying the algebraic part of the axioms of real spectral triples, the axioms postulated in [5]. The construction assumes symmetry under the $\mathcal{U}_q(su(2))$ action and follows the principles of equivariant real spectral triples [15]. The paper also refers to the framework of noncommutative geometry in [4] and to compact quantum-group examples of $q$-deformations in [9].

## 5. Conclusions

The paper states that it obtains an example satisfying the algebraic axioms of real spectral triples, despite the common belief that Connes' approach does not cover quantum-group examples. Alain Connes informed the authors that the dimension axiom can also be satisfied; this concerns the asymptotic behaviour of the eigenvalues of the Dirac operator.

Unlike most other approaches [1,10,11,13], the paper does not assume any form of the Dirac operator beyond equivariance and derives it step by step. Its eigenvalues agree with those in [1], but the paper gives an explicit realisation of all data on the Hilbert space. This includes the representation of the algebra, the proof that the differential forms $[D,\pi(\alpha)]$ have bounded representation, and the reality and chirality operations. Compared with [7,3], the Hilbert space representation and equivariance requirements differ, since the starting algebras differ.

Open problems stated in the paper are the analytic properties of the spectral triple (summability, regularity and finiteness), and the existence of the Hochschild cycle giving the volume form and Poincaré duality; see [2], 10.5. Further planned work includes the classical limit $q\to 1$, the relation of $\mathcal{H}_{\pm}$ and $\mathcal{H}$ to $q$-deformed Hopf bundles [8], and the equivariant Fredholm module obtained from the construction. A systematic analysis of other Podleś spheres, in particular the equatorial quantum sphere, is in preparation [6].
