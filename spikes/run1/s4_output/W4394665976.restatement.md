# Restatement: Introduction (Sections 0.0–0.6, as provided)

Throughout, $X$ denotes a quasicompact quasiseparated scheme unless stated otherwise.

## 0.0 Gal(X)

**0.0.1 Construction.** Define a category $\operatorname{Gal}(X)$ as follows.

- An object is a geometric point $x\to X$: a point $\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(x)\to X$ that exhibits $\kappa(x)$ as a separable closure of the residue field $\kappa(x_0)$ of its image $x_0\in X^{\mathrm{zar}}$.
- Given geometric points $x\to X$ and $y\to X$, a morphism $x\to y$ in $\operatorname{Gal}(X)$ is a specialization $x\leftsquigarrow y$: a lift of the geometric point $y\to X$ to a geometric point $y\to X_{(x)}$ of the strict localization $X_{(x)}\coloneqq\operatorname{Spec}(\mathcal{O}^{\mathrm{sh}}_{X,x_0})$.

The assignment $x\mapsto x_0$ defines a functor from $\operatorname{Gal}(X)$ to the specialization poset of $X^{\mathrm{zar}}$, in which $x_0\le y_0$ if and only if $x_0$ lies in the closure of $y_0$. The fiber over a point $x_0$ is a connected groupoid in which the automorphism group of each object is the absolute Galois group $G_{\kappa(x_0)}$.

**0.0.2 Construction.** For any point $u\to X$ that is finite over its image $u_0\in X^{\mathrm{zar}}$, let $A$ be the unramified extension of the henselization $\mathcal{O}^{\mathrm{h}}_{X,u_0}$ with residue field the separable closure of $\kappa(u_0)$ in $\kappa(u)$, and set $X_{(u)}\coloneqq\operatorname{Spec}A$.

If $v\to X$ is finite over its image $v_0\in X^{\mathrm{zar}}$, a specialization $u\leftsquigarrow v$ is a point $v\to X_{(u)}$ of $X_{(u)}$ lying over $v\to X$.

For any specialization $u\leftsquigarrow v$, let $U(u\leftsquigarrow v)$ be the subset of the set of morphisms of $\operatorname{Gal}(X)$ consisting of those specializations $x\leftsquigarrow y$ that lie over $u\leftsquigarrow v$. The morphisms of $\operatorname{Gal}(X)$ carry the topology generated by the sets $U(u\leftsquigarrow v)$.

## 0.0 Main theorems

Let $\Lambda$ be a coefficient ring. The constructible derived category $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{cons}}(X;\Lambda)$ is understood as an $\infty$-categorical enhancement in each of the following cases.

1. If $\Lambda$ is a finite ring, $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{cons}}(X;\Lambda)$ is the constructible derived category of complexes of $\Lambda$-modules with perfect stalks.
2. If $\Lambda$ is a noetherian ring that is complete with respect to an ideal $I\subset\Lambda$ such that the quotients $\Lambda/I^n$ are finite, $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{cons}}(X;\Lambda)$ is the category of constructible complexes of $\Lambda$-modules with perfect stalks, as constructed by Deligne [183, §1.1], Ekedahl [189], and Bhatt–Scholze [168].
3. If $\Lambda$ is an algebraic extension of $\mathbf{Q}_\ell$, $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{cons}}(X;\Lambda)$ is the category of constructible complexes of $\Lambda$-vector spaces with perfect stalks, as constructed by Deligne [183] and Bhatt–Scholze [168].

In each case the natural topology on $\Lambda$ induces a topology on $\operatorname{Perf}(\Lambda)$. For every $F\in\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{cons}}(X;\Lambda)$, the assignment $x\mapsto F_x$ defines a continuous functor $\rho_F\colon\operatorname{Gal}(X)\to\operatorname{Perf}(\Lambda)$.

**0.0.3 Theorem (Exodromy; 13.0.1, 13.0.4 and 13.0.5).** Let $X$ be a scheme whose underlying topological space is noetherian. Then the assignment $F\mapsto\rho_F$ is an equivalence of $\infty$-categories
$$\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{cons}}(X;\Lambda)\simeq\operatorname{Fun}^{\mathrm{cts}}(\operatorname{Gal}(X),\operatorname{Perf}(\Lambda)).$$
The coefficients may be taken more generally, even nonabelian (see 0.5.2, 0.6.1, 0.6.2 and 0.6.3 for refined statements).

**0.0.4 Theorem (Homotopy; 12.5.1).** Let $X$ be a quasicompact quasiseparated scheme. The map $\theta_X\colon\Pi^{\mathrm{et}}_\infty(X)\to\varepsilon(\operatorname{Gal}(X))$ is an equivalence in an appropriate sense. For each geometric point $x\to X$ and each integer $n\ge 1$, $\theta_X$ induces an isomorphism
$$\pi^{\mathrm{et}}_n(X,x)\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\pi_n(\operatorname{Gal}(X),x).$$

**0.0.5 Theorem (Reconstruction; 14.4.7).** Let $k$ be a finitely generated field of characteristic zero, and let $G_k$ be the absolute Galois group of $k$. Then the assignment $X\mapsto\operatorname{Gal}(X)$ is fully faithful as a functor from normal $k$-varieties to topological categories over $BG_k$ and continuous functors over $BG_k$ that carry minimal objects to minimal objects.

## 0.2 Shape theory

**0.2.2 Example.** Hoyois showed that if $X$ is a locally connected scheme, then the profinite shape $\widehat{\Pi}_\infty(X_{\mathrm{et}})$ of the étale $\infty$-topos $X_{\mathrm{et}}$ coincides with the profinite étale homotopy type $\widehat{\Pi}^{\mathrm{et}}_\infty(X)$ of Artin–Mazur–Friedlander [215, Corollary 5.6].

**0.2.3 Theorem ($\infty$-Categorical Stone Duality; [151, Theorem E.2.4.1]).** The fully faithful functor $\mathbf{S}_\pi\hookrightarrow\mathbf{Top}_\infty$, $\Pi\mapsto\operatorname{Fun}(\Pi,\mathbf{S})$, extends along inverse limits to a fully faithful right adjoint
$$\operatorname{Pro}(\mathbf{S}_\pi)\hookrightarrow\mathbf{Top}_\infty,\qquad\{\Pi_\alpha\}_{\alpha\in A}\mapsto\lim_{\alpha\in A}\operatorname{Fun}(\Pi_\alpha,\mathbf{S}),$$
from profinite spaces to $\infty$-topoi. The left adjoint of this functor is the profinite shape $\widehat{\Pi}_\infty\colon\mathbf{Top}_\infty\to\operatorname{Pro}(\mathbf{S}_\pi)$.

## 0.4 Stratified spaces and Hochster duality

**0.4.2 Definition.** A stratified space $\Pi\to P$ is $\pi$-finite if the poset $P$ is finite, the $\infty$-category $\Pi$ has finitely many objects up to equivalence, and the mapping spaces of $\Pi$ are $\pi$-finite spaces. $\mathbf{Str}_\pi$ denotes the $\infty$-category of $\pi$-finite stratified spaces. Objects of $\operatorname{Pro}(\mathbf{Str}_\pi)$ are called profinite stratified spaces.

**0.4.3 Definition.** Let $S$ be a spectral topological space. An $S$-stratified $\infty$-topos is an $\infty$-topos $\mathbfit{X}$ equipped with a geometric morphism $\mathbfit{X}\to\operatorname{Sh}(S)$ to the $\infty$-topos of sheaves of spaces on $S$. $\mathbf{StrTop}_\infty$ denotes the $\infty$-category of stratified $\infty$-topoi.

**0.4.4 Example.** Let $P$ be a finite poset. Then $\operatorname{Sh}(P)\simeq\operatorname{Fun}(P,\mathbf{S})$, where $\operatorname{Sh}(P)$ is the category of sheaves on the Alexandroff topological space attached to $P$. More generally, if $S$ is a spectral topological space regarded as a profinite poset $\{P_\alpha\}_{\alpha\in A}$, then $\operatorname{Sh}(S)\simeq\lim_{\alpha\in A}\operatorname{Fun}(P_\alpha,\mathbf{S})$.

**0.4.5 Example.** Let $X$ be a coherent scheme. The geometric morphism $X_{\mathrm{et}}\to X_{\mathrm{zar}}=\operatorname{Sh}(X^{\mathrm{zar}})$ is a stratification of the étale $\infty$-topos of $X$ by the Zariski topological space $X^{\mathrm{zar}}$.

**0.4.6 Theorem ($\infty$-Categorical Hochster Duality; 9.3.1).** Write $\widehat{\lambda}\colon\operatorname{Pro}(\mathbf{Str}_\pi)\to\mathbf{StrTop}_\infty$ for the functor carrying a profinite stratified space $\{\Pi_\alpha\}_{\alpha\in A}$ to the $\infty$-topos $\operatorname{Fun}(\Pi,\mathbf{S})\coloneqq\lim_{\alpha\in A}\operatorname{Fun}(\Pi_\alpha,\mathbf{S})$. Then $\widehat{\lambda}$ is fully faithful, and its essential image consists of those bounded coherent stratified $\infty$-topoi in which the truncated coherent objects coincide with the constructible sheaves. These $\infty$-topoi are called spectral $\infty$-topoi (9.2.1).

**0.4.7 Theorem (Exodromy; 10.1.8).** Let $S$ be a spectral topological space. The fully faithful functor $\widehat{\lambda}_S\colon\operatorname{Pro}(\mathbf{Str}_\pi)_S\hookrightarrow\mathbf{StrTop}_{\infty,S}$ admits a left adjoint $\widehat{\Pi}^S_{(\infty,1)}\colon\mathbf{StrTop}_{\infty,S}\to\operatorname{Pro}(\mathbf{Str}_\pi)_S$. For any $S$-stratified $\infty$-topos $\mathbfit{X}$, the unit $\mathbfit{X}\to\operatorname{Fun}(\widehat{\Pi}^S_{(\infty,1)}(\mathbfit{X}),\mathbf{S})$ restricts to an equivalence
$$\operatorname{Fun}(\widehat{\Pi}^S_{(\infty,1)}(\mathbfit{X}),\mathbf{S}_\pi)\simeq\mathbfit{X}^{S\text{-cons}}$$
between the $\infty$-category of representations of $\widehat{\Pi}^S_{(\infty,1)}(\mathbfit{X})$ valued in $\pi$-finite spaces and the $S$-constructible sheaves on $\mathbfit{X}$. The profinite $\infty$-category $\widehat{\Pi}^S_{(\infty,1)}(\mathbfit{X})$ is called the profinite $S$-stratified shape of $\mathbfit{X}$.

**0.4.8 Example (10.1.7).** Let $\mathbfit{X}$ be a spectral $S$-stratified $\infty$-topos. The profinite classifying space of $\widehat{\Pi}^S_{(\infty,1)}(\mathbfit{X})$ is $\widehat{\Pi}_\infty(\mathbfit{X})$.

**0.4.9 Example (10.3.2).** Let $\mathbfit{X}$ be a spectral $S$-stratified $\infty$-topos. The profinite stratified shape $\widehat{\Pi}^S_{(\infty,1)}(\mathbfit{X})$ is a pro-$\infty$-category, and taking the limit of a prosystem defining it recovers the $\infty$-category $\operatorname{Pt}(\mathbfit{X})\coloneqq\operatorname{Fun}^*(S,\mathbfit{X})$ of points of $\mathbfit{X}$.

## 0.5 Exodromy for schemes

**0.5.1 Example.** If $X$ is a coherent scheme, then the stratified $\infty$-topos $X_{\mathrm{et}}\to X_{\mathrm{zar}}$ is spectral. The profinite $\infty$-category $\widehat{\Pi}^{\mathrm{et}}_{(\infty,1)}(X)\coloneqq\widehat{\Pi}^{X^{\mathrm{zar}}}_{(\infty,1)}(X_{\mathrm{et}})$ is the stratified étale homotopy type of $X$. Since the étale $\infty$-topos of $X$ comes from an ordinary topos, $\widehat{\Pi}^{\mathrm{et}}_{(\infty,1)}(X)$ is a profinite $1$-category. The $1$-category $\operatorname{Pt}(X_{\mathrm{et}})$ of points of $X_{\mathrm{et}}$ is the $1$-category $\operatorname{Gal}(X)$ of geometric points of $X$ [280, Exposé VIII, Théorème 7.9]. There is a natural identification
$$\widehat{\Pi}^{\mathrm{et}}_{(\infty,1)}(X)\simeq\operatorname{Gal}(X).$$

**0.5.2 Corollary (Exodromy for schemes).** Let $X$ be a coherent scheme. Then there is a natural equivalence
$$\operatorname{Fun}(\operatorname{Gal}(X),\mathbf{S}_\pi)\simeq X_{\mathrm{et}}^{\mathrm{cons}}.$$

## 0.6 Finite coefficients

**0.6.1 Theorem (Exodromy with finite coefficients; 13.0.1).** Let $X$ be a coherent scheme and let $R$ be a finite ring. Then there is a natural equivalence of $\infty$-categories
$$\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{cons}}(X;R)\simeq\operatorname{Fun}(\operatorname{Gal}(X),\operatorname{Perf}(R)).$$
