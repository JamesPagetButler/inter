# Restatement

## Setting and notation

Throughout, a *category* means an $(\infty,1)$-category, a *topos* means an $\infty$-topos, and $\operatorname{An}$ denotes the category of animae (spaces, homotopy types). For a topological group $G$ (assumed Hausdorff where needed), $\underline{(-)}$ denotes the functor $\operatorname{T1}\operatorname{Top}\to\operatorname{Cond}(\operatorname{Set})$ of the introduction.

**Footnote 3.** A group is *locally profinite* if it is locally compact Hausdorff and totally disconnected.

## Condensed group cohomology (Introduction)

**Definition.**
$$H_{\operatorname{cond}}^{*}(\underline{G},-)\coloneqq\operatorname{Ext}_{\operatorname{Cond}(\mathbb{Z}[\underline{G}])}(\mathbb{Z},-).$$

**Natural transformation.** For a Hausdorff topological group $G$, the Čech-to-cohomology spectral sequence (1.5.13) for the cover $\underline{G}\to *$ yields a natural transformation
$$H_{\operatorname{cont}}^{*}(G,-)\to H_{\operatorname{cond}}^{*}(\underline{G},-)\circ\underline{(-)}.$$

**Cohomology of condensed sets.**
$$\mathbb{H}_{\operatorname{cond}}^{*}(X,-)\coloneqq\operatorname{Ext}^{*}_{\operatorname{Cond}(\operatorname{Ab})}(\mathbb{Z}[X],-).$$

## Main results

**Theorem A (3.4.3, 3.4.5).** Suppose $G$ is a Hausdorff topological group satisfying one of the following:

1. $G$ is homotopy equivalent to a locally compact Hausdorff space.
2. $G$ is homotopy equivalent to a locally contractible topological space.

For a discrete abelian group $M$, viewed as a continuous $G$-module with trivial $G$-action, there is an isomorphism
$$H^{*}_{\operatorname{sing}}(BG,M)\cong\mathbb{H}_{\operatorname{sheaf}}^{*}(BG,M)\cong H_{\operatorname{cond}}^{*}(\underline{G},\underline{M}),$$
where $BG$ is a classifying space of numerable principal $G$-bundles.

**Theorem B (3.5.9).** Suppose $G$ is a Hausdorff topological group which is a finite product of groups of the following types:

1. Groups homotopy equivalent to a coproduct of compact Hausdorff spaces (e.g. locally compact abelian groups and locally profinite groups).
2. Groups homotopy equivalent to a locally contractible space.
3. Groups which are locally connected and locally compact.

Then for all solid continuous $G$-modules $M$,
$$H_{\operatorname{cont}}^{*}(G,M)\cong\operatorname{Ext}^{*}_{\operatorname{Solid}(\mathbb{Z}[\underline{G}])}(\mathbb{Z},\underline{M}).$$

**Theorem C (2.5.19).** Suppose $X$ is a locally compact Hausdorff space and $M$ is a product of a discrete abelian group and a finite-dimensional normed $\mathbb{R}$-vector space. Then
$$\mathbb{H}_{\operatorname{sheaf}}^{*}(X,M)\cong\mathbb{H}_{\operatorname{cond}}^{*}(\underline{X},\underline{M}).$$

**Theorem D (Lemma 2.9.4).** Suppose $M$ is a Hausdorff topological group such that $\underline{M}$ is solid (Definition 2.8.1), e.g. $M$ is discrete or locally profinite. Denote by $M^{\delta}$ the underlying discrete abelian group. Suppose $X$ is a $\operatorname{T1}$ topological space which is homotopy equivalent to a locally contractible space (e.g. a CW-complex). Then there is a natural isomorphism
$$\mathbb{H}_{\operatorname{sheaf}}^{*}(X,M^{\delta})\cong\mathbb{H}_{\operatorname{cond}}^{*}(\underline{X},\underline{M}).$$

**Theorem I (3.4.3).** Suppose $G$ is a Hausdorff topological group and $BG$ is a classifying space for numerable principal $G$-bundles which is $\operatorname{T1}$. (Footnote 5: this exists by Lemma 3.4.1.) For a solid abelian group $M$,
$$H_{\operatorname{cond}}^{*}(\underline{G},\operatorname{triv}M)\cong\mathbb{H}_{\operatorname{cond}}^{*}(\underline{BG},M).$$

## Chapter I: Accessible sheaves and higher algebra in big presentable categories

**Definition 1.2.1 (big topos).** A (large) category arising as a large filtered colimit, in the very large category of large categories, of topoi along fully faithful, left-exact left adjoints is called a *big topos*.

**Definition (big presentable).** A (possibly large) category is *big presentable* if it arises as a (possibly large) filtered colimit, taken in the category of large categories, of presentable categories along fully faithful, left-exact left adjoints.

**Theorem E.** Suppose $\mathcal{B}_{\infty}$ is a big presentable category.

1. The category $\operatorname{Sp}(\mathcal{B}_{\infty})$ of spectrum objects is stable (Lemma 1.3.2) and big presentable (Lemma 1.3.5).
2. The infinite loop space functor $\Omega^{\infty}\colon\operatorname{Sp}(\mathcal{B}_{\infty})\to\mathcal{B}_{\infty}$ admits a left adjoint $\Sigma^{\infty}_{+}\colon\mathcal{B}_{\infty}\to\operatorname{Sp}(\mathcal{B}_{\infty})$, which factors into left adjoints
$$\mathcal{B}_{\infty}\to\operatorname{CMon}(\mathcal{B}_{\infty})\to\operatorname{CGrp}(\mathcal{B}_{\infty})\to\operatorname{Sp}(\mathcal{B}_{\infty})$$
(Lemma 1.3.10).
3. There is a $t$-structure on $\operatorname{Sp}(\mathcal{B}_{\infty})$ whose connective part is generated by the essential image of $\Sigma^{\infty}_{+}$ under small colimits and extensions (Lemma 1.3.12).
4. If $\mathcal{B}_{\infty}$ is a big topos, then $\Sigma^{\infty}_{+}$ factors over an equivalence $\operatorname{CGrp}(\mathcal{B}_{\infty})\cong\operatorname{Sp}(\mathcal{B}_{\infty})_{\geq 0}$.
5. For a stable, big presentable category $\mathcal{C}_{\infty}$, pullback along $\Sigma^{\infty}_{+}$ defines a fully faithful functor
$$(\Sigma^{\infty}_{+})^{*}\colon\operatorname{Fun}^{\operatorname{colim}}(\operatorname{Sp}(\mathcal{B}_{\infty}),\mathcal{C}_{\infty})\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Fun}^{\operatorname{colim}}(\mathcal{B}_{\infty},\mathcal{C}_{\infty}),$$
where $\operatorname{Fun}^{\operatorname{colim}}$ denotes small-colimit-preserving functors. If $\mathcal{B}_{\infty}$ and $\mathcal{C}_{\infty}$ admit small coproducts, then $(\Sigma^{\infty}_{+})^{*}$ is an equivalence (1.3.20).

**Definition 1.3.22.** Big presentably (symmetric) monoidal categories.

**Proposition 1.3.26.** The (symmetric) monoidal structure on a big presentably (symmetric) monoidal category $\mathcal{B}_{\infty}^{\otimes}$ induces a (symmetric) monoidal structure on $\operatorname{Sp}(\mathcal{B}_{\infty})^{\otimes}$ such that $\Sigma^{\infty}_{+}$ enhances to a (symmetric) monoidal functor $\Sigma^{\infty,\otimes}_{+}$.

**Lemma 1.3.28.** For categories of accessible (hyper)sheaves, the monoidal structure of 1.3.26 recovers the closed symmetric monoidal structure induced by the smash product on spectra.

**Lemma F (1.3.30).** For a stable, big presentably (symmetric) monoidal category $\mathcal{C}_{\infty}^{\otimes}$, pullback along $\Sigma^{\infty,\otimes}_{+}\colon\mathcal{B}_{\infty}^{\otimes}\to\operatorname{Sp}(\mathcal{B}_{\infty})^{\otimes}$ induces a fully faithful functor
$$(\Sigma^{\infty,\otimes}_{+})^{*}\colon\operatorname{Fun}^{\operatorname{colim},\otimes}(\operatorname{Sp}(\mathcal{B}_{\infty})^{\otimes},\mathcal{C}_{\infty}^{\otimes})\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Fun}^{\operatorname{colim},\otimes}(\mathcal{B}_{\infty}^{\otimes},\mathcal{C}_{\infty}^{\otimes}).$$
Its essential image consists of the colimit-preserving, symmetric monoidal functors $\mathcal{B}_{\infty}^{\otimes}\to\mathcal{C}_{\infty}^{\otimes}$ which factor as $\mathcal{B}_{\infty}\xrightarrow{\Sigma^{\infty}_{+}}\operatorname{Sp}(\mathcal{B}_{\infty})\to\mathcal{C}_{\infty}$. If $\operatorname{Sp}(\mathcal{B}_{\infty})$ and $\mathcal{C}_{\infty}$ admit small coproducts, then $(\Sigma^{\infty,\otimes}_{+})^{*}$ is an equivalence.

**Definition (cohomology in a big presentable category $\mathcal{X}$, 1.3.35).**
$$\mathbb{H}_{\mathcal{X}}(-,-)\coloneqq\operatorname{map}_{\operatorname{Sp}(\mathcal{X})}(\Sigma^{\infty}_{+}-,-)\colon\mathcal{X}^{\operatorname{op}}\times\operatorname{Sp}(\mathcal{X})\to\operatorname{Sp},$$
$$\mathbb{H}_{\mathcal{X}}^{*}(-,-)\coloneqq\pi_{-*}\mathbb{H}_{\mathcal{X}}(-,-).$$
This admits a Čech-to-cohomology spectral sequence (1.5.13).

**Relation (0.1).** Since every adjunction between stable, big presentable categories is spectrally enriched (1.3.40), every geometric morphism $L\colon\mathcal{X}\rightleftarrows\mathcal{Y}\colon R$ (Definition 1.5.6) between big topoi induces an equivalence
$$\mathbb{H}_{\mathcal{X}}(-,R_{\operatorname{Sp}}-)\cong\mathbb{H}_{\mathcal{Y}}(L-,-),$$
where $R_{\operatorname{Sp}}$ denotes the stabilization (Definition 1.3.3) of $R$.

**Lemma 1.4.9.** For an algebra $R$ in a big presentably monoidal category $\mathcal{C}^{\otimes}$, the category $\operatorname{LMod}_{R}(\mathcal{C})$ of left $R$-modules is big presentable.

**Lemma 1.4.13.** If $\mathcal{C}^{\otimes}$ is big presentably symmetric monoidal and $R\in\operatorname{CAlg}(\mathcal{C})$, then $\operatorname{LMod}_{R}(\mathcal{C})$ inherits the structure of a big presentably symmetric monoidal category.

**Lemma G (1.4.5).** Suppose $\mathcal{C}^{\otimes}$ is a potentially large, closed symmetric monoidal category which has all $\Delta^{\operatorname{op}}$-indexed colimits and all $\Delta$-indexed limits. For $R\in\operatorname{CAlg}(\mathcal{C})$, the induced symmetric monoidal structure (1.4.2) on $\operatorname{LMod}_{R}(\mathcal{C})$ is closed.

**Proposition 1.4.21.** For an algebra $R\in\operatorname{Alg}(\operatorname{Sp}(\mathcal{X})_{\geq 0})$ with $\mathcal{X}$ a big presentably monoidal category, the category of left $R$-modules in $\operatorname{Sp}(\mathcal{X})$ inherits a $t$-structure. If $\mathcal{X}$ is a big topos, its heart is equivalent to the $1$-category $\operatorname{LMod}_{\pi_{0}R}(\operatorname{Ab}(\tau_{\leq 0}\mathcal{X}))$ of underived $\pi_0 R$-modules.

**Lemma 1.4.32.** Conditions on a big topos $\mathcal{X}$ under which
$$\mathcal{D}(\operatorname{LMod}_{\pi_{0}R}(\operatorname{Ab}(\tau_{\leq 0}\mathcal{X})))\cong\operatorname{LMod}_{\pi_{0}R}(\operatorname{Sp}(\mathcal{X})).$$
(Adapting [Lur18a, Theorem 2.1.2.2].)

**Lemma H (2.6.7).** For $R\in\operatorname{Alg}(\operatorname{Cond}_{(\kappa)}(\operatorname{Ab}))$,
$$\mathcal{D}(\operatorname{LMod}_{R}(\operatorname{Cond}_{(\kappa)}(\operatorname{Ab})))\cong\operatorname{LMod}_{R}(\operatorname{Cond}_{(\kappa)}(\operatorname{Sp})).$$

**Cohomology via Ext (Section I).** If $\mathcal{X}$ is a big topos satisfying Lemma 1.4.32, then for $X\in\tau_{\leq 0}\mathcal{X}$ and $A\in\operatorname{Ab}(\tau_{\leq 0}\mathcal{X})\cong\operatorname{Sp}(\mathcal{X})^{\heartsuit}$,
$$\mathbb{H}_{\mathcal{X}}^{*}(X,A)=\operatorname{Ext}_{\operatorname{Ab}(\tau_{\leq 0}\mathcal{X})}^{*}(\mathbb{Z}[X],\mathbb{Z}).$$

## Chapter II: Condensed mathematics

**Remark (categories of condensed animae).** Suppose we work internal to a universe $\mathcal{U}_0$. If $\kappa\in\mathcal{U}_0$ is a small regular strong limit cardinal and $\mathcal{U}_\kappa\subseteq\mathcal{U}_0$ is the associated universe of $\kappa$-small sets, then
$$\operatorname{Cond}_{\kappa}(\operatorname{An})=\operatorname{Pyk}(\operatorname{An})_{\mathcal{U}_0}^{\mathcal{U}_1}.$$
For universes $\mathcal{U}_0\in\mathcal{U}_1\in\mathcal{U}_2$ there are fully faithful left adjoints
$$\operatorname{Cond}(\operatorname{An})_{\mathcal{U}_0}\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Pyk}(\operatorname{An})_{\mathcal{U}_0}^{\mathcal{U}_1}\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Cond}(\operatorname{An})_{\mathcal{U}_1}.$$

**Proposition ([CS19a, Proposition 2.2.7, Section 2.4.2]).**

1. For every uncountable cardinal $\kappa$ there is a right adjoint
$$\underline{(-)}_{\kappa}\colon\operatorname{Top}\to\operatorname{Cond}_{\kappa}(\operatorname{Set}),$$
which is fully faithful on $\kappa$-compactly generated spaces (Definition 2.1.15). Its left adjoint sends a $\kappa$-condensed set $X$ to a $\kappa$-compactly generated topological space with set of points $X(*)$.
2. There is a functor $\underline{(-)}\colon\operatorname{T1}\operatorname{Top}\to\operatorname{Cond}(\operatorname{Set})$ which is fully faithful on compactly generated topological spaces and admits a partially defined left adjoint.

**Comparison map (Section 2.5).** For $\lambda\geq\kappa$, there is a left-exact left adjoint $j^{*}\colon\mathcal{T}^{LS}_{\lambda}\to\operatorname{Cond}_{\kappa}(\operatorname{An})$ from the gros topos of $\lambda$-small topological spaces (Definition 2.5.12), sending $X$ to $\underline{X}_{\kappa}$. Using $j^{*}\dashv j_{*}$ and (0.1), for a $\kappa$-condensed abelian group $A$ there is a comparison map
$$\mathbb{H}_{\operatorname{sheaf}}(X,A_{\kappa})\cong\mathbb{H}_{\mathcal{T}^{LS}_{\lambda}}(X,\pi_{0}j_{*,\operatorname{Sp}}A)\to\mathbb{H}_{\mathcal{T}^{LS}_{\lambda}}(X,j_{*,\operatorname{Sp}}A)\cong\mathbb{H}_{\operatorname{cond}}(\underline{X}_{\kappa},A),$$
where $A_{\kappa}$ is the sheaf $\operatorname{Op}(X)^{\operatorname{op}}\ni U\mapsto\operatorname{Hom}_{\operatorname{Cond}_{\kappa}(\operatorname{Ab})}(\underline{U}_{\kappa},A)$. This map is natural in $X$.

**Statement (2.5.4).** If $X$ is $\operatorname{T1}$ and $\kappa>|X|$ is regular, then
$$\mathbb{H}_{\operatorname{cond}}(\underline{X}_{\kappa},A)\cong\mathbb{H}_{\operatorname{cond}}(\underline{X},A).$$

## Solid modules (Section 2.8)

**Definition 2.8.1.** For a condensed ring $R$, $\operatorname{Solid}_{(\kappa)}(R)\subseteq\operatorname{Cond}_{(\kappa)}(R)$ is the full subcategory of condensed $R$-modules whose underlying condensed abelian group is solid.

The following properties hold:

1. $\operatorname{Solid}_{(\kappa)}(R)$ is an abelian subcategory closed under small limits, colimits and extensions (Lemmas 2.8.15 and 2.8.30), and has enough projectives (2.8.18 and 2.8.45).
2. The forgetful functor $\operatorname{Solid}_{(\kappa)}(R)\to\operatorname{Cond}_{(\kappa)}(R)$ admits a left adjoint $(-)^{\square R}$, called solidification (2.8.16 and 2.8.30). For commutative $R$, $(-)^{\square R}$ is a symmetric monoidal localisation (Lemma 2.8.44).
3. The forgetful functor $\mathcal{D}(\operatorname{Solid}_{(\kappa)}(R))\to\mathcal{D}(\operatorname{Cond}_{(\kappa)}(R))$ admits a left adjoint $(-)^{L\square R}$ in many cases (2.8.61, 2.8.58, 2.8.59, 2.8.23, 2.8.33, 2.8.27). It agrees with the derived functor of solidification where the latter is defined (2.8.35).
4. $\mathcal{D}(\operatorname{Solid}_{(\kappa)}(\mathbb{Z}))\subseteq\mathcal{D}(\operatorname{Cond}_{(\kappa)}(\operatorname{Ab}))$ is a local subcategory and $(-)^{L\square\mathbb{Z}}$ is a symmetric monoidal localisation. The induced symmetric monoidal structure on $\mathcal{D}(\operatorname{Solid}_{(\kappa)})$ is closed (2.8.40).

**Proposition 2.8.56.** For $R$ with the property that the forgetful functor $\mathcal{D}(\operatorname{Solid}_{(\kappa)}(R))\to\mathcal{D}(\operatorname{Solid}_{(\kappa)}(\mathbb{Z}))$ factors over an equivalence
$$\mathcal{D}(\operatorname{Solid}_{(\kappa)}(R))\cong\operatorname{LMod}_{R^{\square\mathbb{Z}}}(\mathcal{D}(\operatorname{Solid}_{(\kappa)}(\mathbb{Z}))),$$
$R$ is called ($\kappa$-)$ss$-flat.

**Lemma 2.8.59.** Condensed rings represented by ($\kappa$-light) profinite topological rings (2.8.60), and group rings $\mathbb{Z}[\underline{G}_{(\kappa)}]$ for Hausdorff topological groups $G$, are ($\kappa$-)$ss$-flat.

**2.8.58.** All light condensed rings are $\aleph_1$-$ss$-flat.

**2.8.61.** ($\kappa$-)$ss$-flatness implies that the forgetful functor $\mathcal{D}(\operatorname{Solid}_{(\kappa)}(R))\to\mathcal{D}(\operatorname{Cond}_{(\kappa)}(R))$ admits a left adjoint $(-)^{L\square R}$.

**2.8.65.** For commutative ($\kappa$-)$ss$-flat condensed rings, $\mathcal{D}(\operatorname{Solid}_{(\kappa)}(R))$ carries a closed symmetric monoidal structure such that $(-)^{L\square R}$ enhances to a symmetric monoidal functor.

**2.8.51, 2.8.53.** Computations of derived and underived solidification of free condensed abelian groups on compact Hausdorff spaces and CW-complexes. These imply that condensed cohomology with solid coefficients is homotopy invariant (2.9.2).

## Chapter III: Group cohomology

**Lemma 3.1.4.** For a group object $G$ in a big topos, the category $\mathcal{X}^{G}$ of $G$-objects is a big topos.

**Definition 3.1.8.** The homotopy orbit functor $(-)//G\colon\mathcal{X}^{G}\to\mathcal{X}$.

**Definition 3.1.19.** $\mathbb{B}G\coloneqq *//G$ denotes the classifying space of principal $\infty$-$G$-bundles in $\mathcal{X}$.

**Lemma 3.2.3.** For a discrete group $G\in\operatorname{Grp}(\tau_{\leq 0}\mathcal{X})$ and $A\in\operatorname{LMod}_{\mathbb{Z}[G]}(\operatorname{Ab}(\tau_{\leq 0}\mathcal{X}))$,
$$H_{\mathcal{X}}^{*}(G,A)=\operatorname{Ext}^{*}_{\operatorname{LMod}_{\mathbb{Z}[G]}(\operatorname{Ab}(\tau_{\leq 0}\mathcal{X}))}(\mathbb{Z},A).$$

**Lemma 3.2.12.** Under mild assumptions on a big topos $\mathcal{X}$, there exists a derived fixed-point functor
$$(-)^{G}\colon\operatorname{LMod}_{\mathbb{S}[G]}(\operatorname{Sp}(\mathcal{X}))\to\operatorname{Sp}(\mathcal{X})$$
refining group cohomology.

**Proposition 3.2.7.** Group cohomology with coefficients in a $G$-module $M$ with trivial $G$-action is isomorphic to the cohomology of $\mathbb{B}G$ with coefficients in $M$.

**[NSS15].** For a group object $G$ in a big topos $\mathcal{X}$, the functor $(-)//G$ exhibits $\mathcal{X}^{G}$ as the classifying topos for principal $\infty$-$G$-bundles, with an equivalence $\mathcal{X}^{G}\cong\mathcal{X}_{/\mathbb{B}G}$.

**Lemma 3.4.2.** For a Hausdorff topological group $G$, every principal $G$-bundle $E\to B$ (with $B$ $\operatorname{T1}$) defines a principal $\infty$-$\underline{G}$-bundle $\underline{E}\to\underline{B}$ in condensed animae, classified by a map $\underline{B}\to\mathbb{B}G$. Pullback along this map yields a comparison map
$$H_{\operatorname{cond}}^{*}(\underline{G},\operatorname{triv}-)\cong\mathbb{H}_{\operatorname{cond}}^{*}(\mathbb{B}\underline{G},-)\to\mathbb{H}_{\operatorname{cond}}^{*}(\underline{B},-).$$

**Remark.** If $0\neq G$, the map $\underline{BG}\to\mathbb{B}G$ classifying a universal numerable principal $G$-bundle $EG\to BG$ is not an equivalence, since $\underline{BG}\in\tau_{\leq 0}\operatorname{Cond}(\operatorname{An})$ whereas $\pi_1\mathbb{B}G=\underline{G}$.

**Footnote 4.** If $\mathcal{X}$ is a big topos as in Lemma 1.4.32 and $\operatorname{Sp}(\mathcal{X})$ has $\Delta^{\operatorname{op}}$-indexed colimits, the Ext-formula of Lemma 3.2.3 applies to $G\in\operatorname{Grp}(\tau_{\leq 0}\mathcal{X})$ and $A\in\operatorname{LMod}_{\mathbb{Z}[G]}(\operatorname{Ab}(\tau_{\leq 0}\mathcal{X}))$.

**Footnote 5.** This exists by Lemma 3.4.1.
