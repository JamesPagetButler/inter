# Restatement

## 1 Introduction

This article constructs $Spin(7)$-instantons on $Spin(7)$-manifolds, which are 8-dimensional Riemannian manifolds with holonomy contained in $Spin(7)$. The construction is carried out on Joyce's $Spin(7)$-manifolds of the second type: resolutions of the quotient of a Calabi–Yau four-orbifold by an anti-holomorphic involution fixing only the singular points.

The group $Spin(7)$ is one of the exceptional cases in Berger's classification [Ber55] of Riemannian holonomy groups of simply connected, irreducible, non-symmetric manifolds; the other exceptional case is $G_2$. Metrics with holonomy $Spin(7)$ were obtained by Bryant [Bry87], by Bryant–Salamon [BS89] in the non-compact case, and by Joyce [Joy96], [Joy99], [Joy00] in the compact case.

Joyce's compact constructions come in two types:

- **(I)** resolutions of $T^8/\Gamma$, where $T^8$ is a torus and $\Gamma$ is a finite group of automorphisms of $T^8$ [Joy96], [Joy00];
- **(II)** resolutions of Calabi–Yau four-orbifolds with finitely many singular points, and an anti-holomorphic involution fixing only the singular points [Joy99], [Joy00].

$Spin(7)$-instantons are Yang–Mills connections on a $Spin(7)$-manifold that minimize the Yang–Mills action. They are higher-dimensional analogues of anti-self-dual instantons in four dimensions. They were discussed by Corrigan–Devchand–Fairlie–Nuyts [CDFN83] and Ward [War84], and in the string theory context by Acharya–O'Loughlin–Spence [AOS97], Baulieu–Kanno–Singer [BKS98], and others. In mathematics they were studied by Reyes Carrión [RC98], Lewis [Lew98], Donaldson–Thomas [DT98], Donaldson–Segal [DS09], and others. Analytic results on gauge theory in higher dimensions appear in Nakajima [Nak88], [Nak87], Tian [Tia00], Brendle [Bre03a], [Bre03b], Tao–Tian [TT04], and others.

Lewis [Lew98] constructed $Spin(7)$-instantons on $Spin(7)$-manifolds of type (I), by gluing anti-self-dual instantons on $\mathbb{R}^4$ along a Cayley submanifold. In this article, $Spin(7)$-instantons are constructed on $Spin(7)$-manifolds of type (II). Assuming Hermitian–Einstein connections on the Calabi–Yau four-orbifold and on the ALE spaces, subject to certain conditions, these are glued to obtain a $Spin(7)$-instanton on the manifold (Theorem 6.1).

**Organization.** Section 2 outlines Joyce's construction. Section 3 introduces the $Spin(7)$-instanton equation, its relation to the complex ASD equation and the Hermitian–Einstein equation, and the linearization of these equations. Section 4 constructs approximate solutions and derives an estimate. Section 5 discusses the linearized operator and estimates from its Fredholm property. Section 6 gives the construction using the estimates of Sections 4 and 5. Section 7 gives an example.

**Notation.** $C$ denotes a positive constant independent of $t$, where $t$ is the gluing parameter introduced in Section 2.3. Its value may change from occurrence to occurrence.

## 2 Joyce's Construction

**Proposition 2.1** ([Joy00], Proposition 10.5.3). Let $M$ be an eight-manifold with a $Spin(7)$-structure $(\Omega, g)$. The following are equivalent:

1. $\mathrm{Hol}(g) \subset Spin(7)$;
2. $\nabla\Omega = 0$;
3. $d\Omega = 0$.

**Theorem 2.2** ([Joy00], Theorem 10.6.8). Let $M$ be a compact $Spin(7)$-manifold with torsion-free $Spin(7)$-structure $(\Omega, g)$. Then $\mathrm{Hol}(g) = Spin(7)$ if and only if $\pi_1(M) = 0$ and $\hat{A}(M) = 1$.

**Example 2.3** ([Joy00], pp. 406–407). Consider the hypersurface of degree 12 in the weighted projective space $\mathbb{CP}^5_{1,1,1,1,4,4}$ given by
$$
[z_0:z_1:z_2:z_3:z_4:z_5] \in \mathbb{CP}^5_{1,1,1,1,4,4} : \; z_0^{12}+z_1^{12}+z_2^{12}+z_3^{12}+z_4^{3}+z_5^{3}=0.
$$
Then $c_1(Y)=0$, so $Y$ is a Calabi–Yau four-orbifold. It has three singular points
$$
p_1=[0,0,0,0,1,-1],\quad p_2=[0,0,0,0,1,e^{i\pi/3}],\quad p_3=[0,0,0,0,1,e^{-i\pi/3}].
$$
Define $\sigma: Y \to Y$ by
$$
[z_0:z_1:z_2:z_3:z_4:z_5] \mapsto [\bar z_1 : -\bar z_0 : \bar z_3 : -\bar z_2 : \bar z_5 : \bar z_4].
$$
Then $\sigma$ is an anti-holomorphic involution that fixes only the singular points $p_1, p_2, p_3$.

**Theorem 2.4** ([Joy00], Theorem 13.6.1, Proposition 13.7.1). Let $\lambda, \mu, \nu > 0$ be constants. Then there exist constants $\kappa, K > 0$ such that for $0 < t \le \kappa$ the following holds. Let $M$ be a compact 8-manifold and $(\Omega^t, g^t)$ a $Spin(7)$-structure on $M$. Suppose $\phi^t$ is a four-form on $M$ with $d\Omega^t + d\phi^t = 0$, and:

- (i) $\|\phi^t\|_{L^2} \le \lambda t^{13/3}$ and $\|d\phi^t\|_{L^{10}} \le \lambda t^{7/5}$;
- (ii) the injectivity radius $\delta(g^t)$ satisfies $\delta(g^t) \ge \mu t$;
- (iii) the Riemannian curvature satisfies $\|R(g^t)\|_{C^0} \le \nu t^{-2}$.

Then there exists a smooth torsion-free $Spin(7)$-structure $(\tilde\Omega^t, \tilde g^t)$ on $M$ such that
$$
\|\tilde\Omega^t - \Omega^t\|_{C^0} \le K t^{1/3}, \qquad \|\nabla(\tilde\Omega^t - \Omega^t)\|_{L^{10}} \le K t^{2/15}.
$$

## 5 Linearization and Estimates

**Proposition 5.1** (Sobolev embedding ([Loc87], Theorem 4.8)). Let $k \ge l \ge 0$ and $p, q \ge 1$. If $\frac1p \le \frac1q + \frac{k-l}{n}$ and $\delta \le \delta'$, then $L^p_{k,\delta}(E) \to L^q_{l,\delta'}(E)$ is a continuous inclusion.

**Proposition 5.2.** Let $X$ be an ALE $Spin(7)$-manifold, $E$ a unitary vector bundle over $X$, and $A$ a $Spin(7)$-instanton on $E$ asymptotic to a flat connection $A_0$ at infinity. Let $p \ge 1$, $k \ge 0$, and $\delta \in \mathbb{R} \setminus \mathcal{D}$. Then the operator
$$
L_A : L^p_{k+1,\delta}(\mathfrak{u}(E)\otimes\Lambda^1) \to L^p_{k,\delta-1}(\mathfrak{u}(E)\otimes(\Lambda^0\oplus\Lambda^2_7))
$$
is Fredholm. Moreover, the kernel, cokernel, and index are independent of $p$, $k$, and $\delta$ within any connected component of $\mathbb{R}\setminus\mathcal{D}$.

**Proposition 5.3.** Let $X$ be an ALE $Spin(7)$-manifold, $E$ a unitary vector bundle over $X$, and $A_0$ a connection asymptotic at rate $\lambda$ to a flat connection at infinity. Assume $a \in L^8_{k+1,\mu}(\mathfrak{u}(E)\otimes\Lambda^1)$ with $\mu < -1$, and that $A_0 + a$ satisfies the $Spin(7)$-instanton equation with $d^*_{A_0} a = 0$. Then $a \in L^8_{k+1,\mu'}(\mathfrak{u}(E)\otimes\Lambda^1)$ for any $\mu'$ with $\lambda \le \mu' < \mu$ satisfying $[\mu', \mu] \cap \mathcal{D} = \emptyset$.

**Lemma 5.4.** Let $a \in L^8_{k+1,\delta}(\mathfrak{u}(E)\otimes\Lambda^1)$ with $\delta < -1$. Then for $\varepsilon \ge 2\delta + 1$ with $\varepsilon \in \mathbb{R}\setminus\mathcal{D}$, there exists $\beta \in L^8_{k+1,\varepsilon}(\mathfrak{u}(E)\otimes\Lambda^1)$ such that $L_{A_0}\beta = (0, \pi_7^2(a\wedge a))$ holds near infinity.

**Lemma 5.5.** There exists a constant $C_2 > 0$ such that the following holds for any $a_{X_{n_j}} \in L^4_{1,-1}(\mathfrak{u}(E_{X_{n_j}})\otimes\Lambda^1)$ that is $L^2$-orthogonal to $K_{X_{n_j}}$:
$$
\|a_{X_{n_j}}\|_{L^8} + \|\nabla a_{X_{n_j}}\|_{L^4} \le C_2 \|L_{A_{X_{n_j}}} a_{X_{n_j}}\|_{L^4}. \tag{5.1}
$$

**Lemma 5.6.** There exists a constant $C_3 > 0$ such that the following holds for any $a_Z \in L^4_1(\mathfrak{u}(E_Z)\otimes\Lambda^1)$ that is $L^2$-orthogonal to $K_Z$:
$$
\|a_Z\|_{L^8} + \|\nabla a_Z\|_{L^4} \le C_3 \|L_{A_Z} a_Z\|_{L^4}. \tag{5.2}
$$

**Proposition 5.7.** There exists a constant $C_4 > 0$, independent of $t$, such that if $t$ is sufficiently small and $a \in L^4_1(\mathfrak{u}(E)\otimes\Lambda^1)$ is $L^2$-orthogonal to $K$, then
$$
\|a\|_{L^8} + \|\nabla a\|_{L^4} \le C_4 \|L_{A_t} a\|_{L^4},
$$
where $L_{A_t}$ is the linearized operator with respect to the $Spin(7)$-structure $\Omega^t$ on $M$.

**Proposition 5.8.** There exists a constant $C_5 > 0$, independent of $t$, such that if $a \in L^4_1(\mathfrak{u}(E)\otimes\Lambda^1)$ is $L^2$-orthogonal to $K$, then for $t$ sufficiently small
$$
\|a\|_{L^8} + \|\nabla a\|_{L^4} \le C_5 \|\tilde L_{A_t} a\|_{L^4},
$$
where $\tilde L_{A_t}$ is the linearized operator with respect to the torsion-free $Spin(7)$-structure $\tilde\Omega^t$ on $M$.

**Corollary 5.9.**
$$
\ker \tilde L_{A_t} \cap K^\perp = \{0\}.
$$
Hence
$$
\dim\ker\tilde L_{A_t} \le \dim K = \dim\ker L_Z + \sum_{j=1}^k \dim\ker L_{X_{n_j}}.
$$

**Lemma 5.10.** There exists a constant $C > 0$ such that the following holds for any $(a_{X_{n_j}}, b_{X_{n_j}}) \in L^4_{1,-1}\big(\mathfrak{u}(E_{X_{n_j}})\otimes(\Lambda^0(X_{n_j})\oplus\Lambda^2_7(X_{n_j}))\big)$:
$$
\|a_{X_{n_j}}\|_{L^8} + \|\nabla a_{X_{n_j}}\|_{L^4} + \|b_{X_{n_j}}\|_{L^8} + \|\nabla b_{X_{n_j}}\|_{L^4} \le C\|L^*_{A_{X_{n_j}}}(a_{X_{n_j}}, b_{X_{n_j}})\|_{L^4}.
$$

**Lemma 5.11.** There exists a constant $C > 0$ such that the following holds for any $(a_Z, b_Z) \in L^4_1\big(\mathfrak{u}(E_Z)\otimes(\Lambda^0(Z)\oplus\Lambda^2_7(Z))\big)$ with $a_Z \perp C_Z$:
$$
\|a_Z\|_{L^8} + \|\nabla a_Z\|_{L^4} + \|b_Z\|_{L^8} + \|\nabla b_Z\|_{L^4} \le C\|L^*_{A_Z}(a_Z, b_Z)\|_{L^4}.
$$

**Proposition 5.12.** There exists a constant $C_7 > 0$, independent of $t$, such that if $(a,b) \in L^4_1\big(\mathfrak{u}(E)\otimes(\Lambda^0(M)\oplus\Lambda^2_7(M))\big)$ with $a \perp C_Z$, then
$$
\|a\|_{L^8} + \|\nabla a\|_{L^4} + \|b\|_{L^8} + \|\nabla b\|_{L^4} \le C_7 \|\tilde L^*_{A_t}(a,b)\|_{L^4}.
$$

**Proposition 5.13.**
$$
L^4\big(\mathfrak{u}(E)\otimes(\Lambda^0\oplus\Lambda^2_7)\big) = C_Z \oplus \tilde L_{A_t}\big(K^\perp \cap L^4_1(\mathfrak{u}(E)\otimes\Lambda^1)\big). \tag{5.8}
$$

## 6 Construction of $Spin(7)$-Instantons

**Theorem 6.1.** Let $M = M^t$ be the torsion-free $Spin(7)$-manifold of Section 2. That is, $M$ is a desingularization of a Calabi–Yau four-orbifold $Y$ with finitely many singular points and an anti-holomorphic involution fixing only the singular set, obtained by gluing ALE $Spin(7)$-manifolds $X_{n_j}$ ($j = 1, \dots, k$) at the singular points $p_j$ ($j = 1, \dots, k$). Assume there are Hermitian–Einstein connections on $Y$ and on the $W_{n_j}$ satisfying the conditions in Section 4.1. Then there exists a $Spin(7)$-instanton on a vector bundle $E$ over $M = M^t$ for $t$ sufficiently small.

**Lemma 6.2.** Assume $e \in L^4(\mathfrak{u}(E)\otimes\Lambda^2_7)$ and $c \in C_Z$ satisfy $(c, e) \in \tilde L_{A_t}(K^\perp)$. Then
$$
\|c\|_{L^4_1} \le C_8\|e\|_{L^4}. \tag{6.3}
$$

**Lemma 6.3.** There exists a constant $C_9 > 0$, independent of $t$, such that for all $k$ and sufficiently small $t$:
$$
\|a_t^k\|_{L^8} \le C_9 t^{1/3}, \tag{6.7}
$$
$$
\|a_t^k - a_t^{k-1}\|_{L^8} \le C_9 t^{1/3} 2^{-k}. \tag{6.8}
$$

**Proposition 6.4.** For $t$ sufficiently small, there exist $a_t \in L^4_1(\mathfrak{u}(E)\otimes\Lambda^1)$ with $\|a_t\|_{L^8} \le C_9 t^{1/3}$, and $c_t \in C_Z$ with $\|c_t\|_{L^4_1} \le C_{10} t^{1/3}$, such that $A_t + a_t$ satisfies the $Spin(7)$-instanton equation and $d^*_{A_t} a_t = c_t$.

**Lemma 6.5.** If $A + a \in L^4_1(\mathfrak{u}(E)\otimes\Lambda^1)$ is a $Spin(7)$-instanton with $\|a\|_{L^8}$ sufficiently small and $\|d_A^* a\|_{L^4_1}$ bounded, then $a \in L^4_2(\mathfrak{u}(E)\otimes\Lambda^1)$.

**Remark 6.6.** By the Sobolev embedding theorem, if $a \in L^4_2$ then $a \in L^8_1$. Since $Spin(7)$-instantons are Yang–Mills connections, results on Yang–Mills connections such as those in [Uhl82a], [Uhl82b], and [Weh04] can be used. For example, Theorem 9.4 in [Weh04] gives a gauge transformation $g$ such that $g^*(a)$ is smooth.
