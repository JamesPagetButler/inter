# Restatement

## Abstract

We study the special algebraic properties of alternating 3-forms in 6 and 7 dimensions and introduce a diffeomorphism-invariant functional on the space of differential 3-forms on a closed manifold $M$ in these dimensions. Restricting the functional to closed forms in a fixed cohomology class, we find that a critical point which is generic in a suitable sense defines in the 6-dimensional case a complex threefold with trivial canonical bundle and in 7 dimensions a Riemannian manifold with holonomy $G_2$. This approach gives a direct method of finding a local moduli space, with its special geometry, for these structures.

## Introduction

The introduction states the following mathematical relations.

- Let $W$ be a real vector space of dimension 6 or 7. The group $GL(W)$ has an open orbit on $\Lambda^3 W^*$.
- For $\dim W = 6$ the stabilizer of a point in the open orbit is conjugate to $SL(3,\mathbf{C})$. For $\dim W = 7$ it is conjugate to $G_2$.
- A 3-form $\Omega$ on $M$ defines a reduction of the structure group of $M$ to one of these groups.
- The functional $\Phi$ is the integral of a volume form defined algebraically by a 3-form. It is diffeomorphism-invariant.
- Every critical point of $\Phi$ lies on an orbit of critical points, so $\Phi$ is never Morse. Formally, $\Phi$ is Morse–Bott: its Hessian is non-degenerate transverse to the orbits of $\mathrm{Diff}(M)$. In the 6-dimensional case this assumes the $\partial\bar\partial$-lemma for the complex threefold. In the $G_2$ case an analogue of this lemma is needed, which is essentially contained in [7].
- The moduli space of complex structures together with holomorphic 3-forms on a 6-manifold is locally an open set in $H^3(M,\mathbf{R})$.
- The complex structure on this moduli space carries a special pseudo-Kähler structure.
- For $G_2$-manifolds the moduli space is isomorphic to an open set in $H^3(M,\mathbf{R})$ with an indefinite metric of Hessian type, completing a line of thought due to Joyce [7].
- Section structure: Section 2 treats $\Lambda^3 W^*$ for $\dim W = 6$, where over $\mathbf{C}$ a generic 3-form is a sum of two decomposable ones. Section 3 treats $\Lambda^3 W^*$ as a symplectic vector space under $SL(W)$. Section 4 treats self-duality for a metric of signature $(5,1)$. Section 5 introduces the invariant functional on 3-forms on a 6-manifold and relates critical points to integrable complex structures. Section 6 proves Morse–Bott nondegeneracy formally and rigorously in a Sobolev space model. Section 7 treats $G_2$-manifolds.

## Definitions, Propositions, Theorems and Lemmas

**Definition 1.** Define $\lambda(\Omega)\in(\Lambda^6V^*)^2$ by
$$\lambda(\Omega)=\tfrac{1}{6}\,\mathrm{tr}\,K_\Omega^2.$$

**Proposition 1.** For $\Omega\in\Lambda^3V^*$, $\lambda(\Omega)\neq 0$ if and only if $\Omega=\alpha+\beta$ where $\alpha,\beta$ are decomposable and $\alpha\wedge\beta\neq 0$. The 3-forms $\alpha,\beta$ are unique up to ordering.

**Proposition 2.** Suppose $\lambda(\Omega)\neq 0$ for $\Omega\in\Lambda^3W^*$. Then:
- $\lambda(\Omega)>0$ if and only if $\Omega=\alpha+\beta$ where $\alpha,\beta$ are real decomposable 3-forms and $\alpha\wedge\beta\neq 0$.
- $\lambda(\Omega)<0$ if and only if $\Omega=\alpha+\bar\alpha$ where $\alpha\in\Lambda^3(W^*\otimes\mathbf{C})$ is a complex decomposable 3-form and $\alpha\wedge\bar\alpha\neq 0$.

**Definition 2.** Let $W$ be oriented and $\Omega\in\Lambda^3W^*$ with $\lambda(\Omega)\neq 0$. Writing $\Omega$ in terms of decomposables ordered by the orientation, define $\hat\Omega\in\Lambda^3W^*$ by:
- if $\lambda(\Omega)>0$ and $\Omega=\alpha+\beta$, then $\hat\Omega=\alpha-\beta$;
- if $\lambda(\Omega)<0$ and $\Omega=\alpha+\bar\alpha$, then $\hat\Omega=i(\bar\alpha-\alpha)$.

**Proposition 3.** The moment map for $SL(W)$ acting on $\Lambda^3W^*$ is
$$\mu(\Omega)=K_\Omega.$$

**Proposition 4.** Let $\Omega\mapsto\hat\Omega$ be the transformation of Definition 2. Then
$$X_\phi(\Omega)=-\hat\Omega.$$

**Proposition 5.** The derivative $J=DX_\phi\in\mathrm{End}(\Lambda^3W^*)$ of $X_\phi:U\to\Lambda^3W^*$ at $\Omega$ satisfies $J^2=-1$ and defines an integrable complex structure on $U$.

**Proposition 6.** The complex structure $J$ equals $i$ on $\Lambda^{3,0}\oplus\Lambda^{2,1}$ and $-i$ on $\Lambda^{1,2}\oplus\Lambda^{0,3}$.

**Definition 3.** A special Kähler manifold is a complex manifold $M$ with complex structure $J\in\Omega^1(T)$ such that:
- there is a Kähler metric $g$ with Kähler form $\omega$;
- there is a flat torsion-free connection $D$ such that $D\omega=0$ and $d_DJ=0\in\Omega^2(M,T)$.

**Proposition 7.** The open set $U\subset\Lambda^3W^*$ has an $SL(W)$-invariant special pseudo-Kähler structure of Hermitian signature $(1,9)$, and $X_\phi$ is an infinitesimal isometry of the metric.

**Proposition 8.** Let $\Omega\in\Lambda^3W^*$ be self-dual. Then $\lambda(\Omega)\geq 0$. If $\lambda(\Omega)>0$, then $\Omega=\alpha+\ast\alpha$ where $\alpha\in\Lambda^3W^*$ is decomposable.

**Proposition 9.** If $\Omega$ is self-dual, then $\hat\Omega$ is anti-self-dual.

**Proposition 10.** The subspaces $\Lambda_+,\Lambda_-\subset\Lambda^3W^*$ of self-dual and anti-self-dual 3-forms are Lagrangian.

**Proposition 11.** The map $\Omega\mapsto\hat\Omega$ from $\Lambda_+$ to $\Lambda_-\cong\Lambda_+^*$ is the derivative of $-\phi$ restricted to $\Lambda_+$.

**Proposition 12.**
- If $A\in Sym^2S^*$ represents a self-dual 3-form $\Omega$, then $\hat\Omega=\sqrt{\det A}\,A^{-1}\in Sym^2S$.
- If $B\in Sym^2S$ represents an anti-self-dual 3-form $\Omega$, then $\hat\Omega=-\sqrt{\det A}\,A^{-1}\in Sym^2S$.

**Theorem 13.** Let $M$ be a compact complex 3-manifold with trivial canonical bundle and $\Omega$ the real part of a non-vanishing holomorphic 3-form. Then $\Omega$ is a critical point of the functional $\Phi$ restricted to the cohomology class $[\Omega]\in H^3(M,\mathbf{R})$.

Conversely, if $\Omega$ is a critical point on a cohomology class of an oriented closed 6-manifold $M$ and $\lambda(\Omega)<0$ everywhere, then $\Omega$ defines on $M$ the structure of a complex manifold such that $\Omega$ is the real part of a non-vanishing holomorphic 3-form.

**Proposition 14.** Let $M$ be a compact complex 3-manifold with non-vanishing holomorphic 3-form $\Omega+i\hat\Omega$. Suppose $M$ satisfies the $\partial\bar\partial$-lemma. Then the Hessian of $\Phi$ is non-degenerate transverse to the action of $\mathrm{Diff}(M)$ at $\Omega$.

**Lemma 15.** Let $M$ be a compact complex 3-manifold with non-vanishing holomorphic 3-form which satisfies the $\partial\bar\partial$-lemma. Then $d\psi$ is tangent to the $\mathrm{Diff}(M)$-orbit of $\Omega$ if and only if $dJd\psi=0$.

**Lemma 16.** If $\gamma\in L^2_{k+1}(\Lambda^{1,1})$ is a real form satisfying $(d^*d\gamma)^{2,0}=0$, then there exist a real form $\rho\in L^2_k(\Lambda^3)$ with $d^*\rho=0$ and a complex form $\sigma\in L^2_{k+1}(\Lambda^{2,2})$ such that
$$d\gamma=\rho+\partial^*\sigma+\bar\partial^*\bar\sigma.$$

**Proposition 17.** The open set $U\subset H^3(M,\mathbf{R})$ has the structure of a special pseudo-Kähler manifold of Hermitian signature $(1,h^{2,1}-1)$.

**Definition 4.** Fix a basis vector $\epsilon\in\Lambda^7W^*$. For a positive form $\Omega$, define $\phi(\Omega)\in\mathbf{R}$ by
$$\phi(\Omega)\,\epsilon=\det K_\Omega^{1/9}.$$

**Proposition 18.** The derivative of $\phi$ is
$$d\phi(\dot\Omega)\,\epsilon=\tfrac{7}{18}\,\ast\Omega\wedge\dot\Omega,$$
where $\ast:\Lambda^3W^*\to\Lambda^4W^*$ is the Hodge star operator of the metric $g_\Omega$.

**Theorem 19.** Let $M$ be a closed 7-manifold with a metric with holonomy $G_2$, with defining 3-form $\Omega$. Then $\Omega$ is a critical point of the functional $\Phi$ restricted to the cohomology class $[\Omega]\in H^3(M,\mathbf{R})$.

Conversely, if $\Omega$ is a critical point on a cohomology class of a closed oriented 7-manifold $M$ such that $\Omega$ is everywhere positive, then $\Omega$ defines on $M$ a metric with holonomy $G_2$.

**Lemma 20.** The derivative of the map $\Theta(\Omega)=\ast\Omega$ at $\Omega$ is
$$D\Theta(\dot\Omega)=\tfrac{4}{3}\,\ast\pi_1(\dot\Omega)+\ast\pi_7(\dot\Omega)-\ast\pi_{27}(\dot\Omega).$$

**Proposition 21.** Let $M$ be a compact 7-manifold with a closed 3-form $\Omega$ which defines a $G_2$-structure. Then the Hessian of $\Phi$ at $\Omega$ is non-degenerate transverse to the action of $\mathrm{Diff}(M)$ at $\Omega$.

**Proposition 22.** Let $M$ be an irreducible $G_2$-manifold and $U\subset H^3(M,\mathbf{R})$ a local moduli space. Then the Hessian of the function $\Phi([\Omega])$ defines a metric on $U$ of signature $(1,b_3-1)$.
