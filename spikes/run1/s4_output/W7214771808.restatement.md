# Restatement

## Abstract

We prove that the sheaf $\infty$-topos associated to a geometric site in the sense of Lurie can be written as a partially lax limit of smaller sheaf $\infty$-topoi. As a consequence, the $\infty$-topos of $\kappa$-small condensed anima is a partially lax limit of sheaf $\infty$-topoi over extremally disconnected spaces, with the open embeddings marked. We also deduce a gros version of the étale exodromy theorem.

## Introduction: statements and mathematical relations

**Geometric sites.** A geometric site consists of a site $(\mathcal{G},\tau)$ together with a family of admissible morphisms $\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{ad}}\subset\mathcal{G}$ satisfying compatibilities, see Definition 2.1. Open embeddings in $\operatorname{Top}$ form a class of admissible morphisms.

**Pullback and sheaves on slices.** Pullback along $f\colon X\to Y$ in $\mathcal{G}$ gives a morphism of sites $f^*\colon \mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{ad}}_{/Y}\to\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{ad}}_{/X}$. The functor $\operatorname{Sh}(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{ad}}_{/Y};\mathcal{C})\to\operatorname{Sh}(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{ad}}_{/X};\mathcal{C})$ is obtained by left Kan extension along $(f^*)^{\mathrm{op}}$ on presheaves, followed by sheafification $L_\tau$. The functor $\operatorname{Sh}(\mathcal{G};\mathcal{C})\to\operatorname{Sh}(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{ad}}_{/X};\mathcal{C})$ on the $X$-factor of Theorem A is restriction along the forgetful functor $\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{ad}}_{/X}\to\mathcal{G}$. Section 4 and Section 5 construct these functors using $(\infty,2)$-categories. The conventions on $(\infty,2)$-categories follow [BB25, Appendix A].

**Presheaves.** Write $\mathcal{P}^{\mathcal{C}}(-)\coloneqq\operatorname{Fun}((-)^{\mathrm{op}},\mathcal{C})$.

**Fractured $\infty$-topoi.** Following Lurie [LUR18, Section 20], a fractured $\infty$-topos is a subcategory $\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{corp}}$ of an $\infty$-topos $\mathcal{X}$ satisfying compatibilities. For a geometric site, [LUR18, Theorem 20.6.3.4] states that $\operatorname{Sh}(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{ad}})\subseteq\operatorname{Sh}(\mathcal{G})$ is a fractured $\infty$-topos. Here $\operatorname{Sh}(\mathcal{G})$ plays the role of the gros topos and $\{\operatorname{Sh}(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{ad}}_{/X})\}_{X\in\mathcal{G}}$ plays the role of the petit topoi.

**Partially lax limits.** Partially lax limits are introduced in [GHN17].

**Explicit data.** A sheaf $\mathcal{F}\in\operatorname{Sh}(\mathcal{G};\mathcal{C})$ is equivalent to the following data:
- a sheaf $\mathcal{F}_X\in\operatorname{Sh}(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{ad}}_{/X};\mathcal{C})$ for each $X\in\mathcal{G}$;
- a compatible family of morphisms $f_\alpha\colon \alpha^*\mathcal{F}_Y\to\mathcal{F}_X$ for every $\alpha\colon X\to Y$ in $\mathcal{G}$, where $f_\alpha$ is an equivalence whenever $\alpha$ is admissible.

This equivalence is compatible with the pointwise symmetric monoidal structure (Remark 3.6).

**Simplification.** Certain functors send partially lax limits to limits (Section 3.2). If $\mathcal{G}$ has a terminal object $\ast$, then $\lim_{\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{op}}}$ is computed by evaluation at $\ast$. In this case the restriction functor $\operatorname{Sh}(\mathcal{G};\mathcal{C})\to\operatorname{Sh}(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{ad}}_{/\ast};\mathcal{C})$ induces equivalences on:
1. full subcategories of dualizable objects;
2. Picard spectra, Picard spaces and Picard groups;
3. rigidifications in the sense of Gaitsgory–Rozenblyum [GR17, HSS+21, RAM26].

See Corollary 5.5.

**Condensed objects.** Fix an uncountable strong limit cardinal $\kappa$. Let $\operatorname{ExtrDisc}$ be the $1$-category of $\kappa$-small extremally disconnected compact Hausdorff spaces, i.e. $\kappa$-small compact Hausdorff spaces in which the closure of every open set is open. Every such space is profinite. Equivalently, these are the projective objects of $\operatorname{CHaus}$ [GLE58, Theorem 2.5]. $\operatorname{ExtrDisc}$ carries the Grothendieck topology of finitely jointly surjective families. For a cocomplete $\infty$-category $\mathcal{C}$, condensed objects are defined by
$$\operatorname{Cond}(\mathcal{C})\coloneqq\operatorname{Sh}(\operatorname{ExtrDisc};\mathcal{C})\simeq\operatorname{Fun}^{\times}(\operatorname{ExtrDisc}^{\mathrm{op}};\mathcal{C}),$$
as in [BH19, SCH26b, SCH26a].

**Previous work.** [RZ26, Theorems A and B] identify $\operatorname{Cond}(\operatorname{An})$ as a fractured $\infty$-topos presented by a geometric site and compute its petit topoi.

**Facts about $\operatorname{Sh}(X;\operatorname{Sp})$.** For $X\in\operatorname{ExtrDisc}$, the symmetric monoidal category $\operatorname{Sh}(X;\operatorname{Sp})$ is compactly generated [HAR25, Proposition 3.1] and rigid [HSS+21, Proposition 2.20]. Its dualizable objects are precisely the compact objects. Equivalences in $\operatorname{Sh}(X;\operatorname{Sp})$ are detected on the points of $X$.

**Étale exodromy.** Van Dobben de Bruyn, building on [BGH20, WOL22], proves an exodromy correspondence for Postnikov complete étale sheaves. It identifies sheaves on a scheme with continuous functors out of a Galois $\infty$-category [VAN26, Theorem 2]. The condensed Galois $\infty$-category of $X$ (see [BGH20, HHL+25, MAI26, VAN26]) is
$$\operatorname{Gal}(X)\colon\operatorname{ExtrDisc}^{\mathrm{op}}\to\operatorname{Cat}_\infty,\qquad S\mapsto\operatorname{Fun}^{*}_{\mathrm{loc\,coh}}(\operatorname{Sh}(X_{\text{ét}}),\operatorname{Sh}(S)),$$
where for $S\in\operatorname{ExtrDisc}^{\mathrm{op}}$ this is the $\infty$-category of locally coherent geometric morphisms $\operatorname{Sh}(S)\to\operatorname{Sh}(X_{\text{ét}})$ [VAN26, 2.1.6]. It is functorial in $X$. The gros version is
$$\operatorname{GAL}\colon\operatorname{ExtrDisc}^{\mathrm{op}}\to\operatorname{Cat}_\infty,\qquad S\mapsto\operatorname*{laxcolim^{\dagger}}_{X\in\operatorname{Sch}}\operatorname{Gal}(X)(S),$$
where the étale maps are marked and $X$ runs over $\kappa$-small qcqs schemes (Definition 6.3). The condensed $\infty$-category associated to $\mathcal{C}$ is $\underline{\mathcal{C}}=\operatorname{Sh}(-;\mathcal{C})\colon\operatorname{ExtrDisc}^{\mathrm{op}}\to\operatorname{Cat}_\infty$.

## Labelled statements

**Theorem A (Theorem 5.2).** Let $(\mathcal{G},\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{ad}},\tau)$ be a geometric site and $\mathcal{C}$ a presentably symmetric monoidal $\infty$-category. There is an equivalence of symmetric monoidal $\infty$-categories
$$\operatorname{Sh}(\mathcal{G};\mathcal{C})\xrightarrow{\;\simeq\;}\operatorname*{laxlim^{\dagger}}_{X\in\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{op}}}\operatorname{Sh}(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{ad}}_{/X};\mathcal{C}),$$
where the partially lax limit is indexed by the marked $\infty$-category $(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{ad}})^{\mathrm{op}}\subset\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{op}}$. The monoidal structure is the sheafification of the pointwise monoidal structure.

**Theorem B (Theorem 4.4).** Let $\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{ad}}\subset\mathcal{G}$ be a pullback-stable left-cancellative wide subcategory of a small $\infty$-category $\mathcal{G}$. Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a cocomplete symmetric monoidal $\infty$-category such that the monoidal structure commutes with colimits in each variable. There is a preferred symmetric monoidal equivalence of $\infty$-categories
$$\mathcal{P}^{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{G})\xrightarrow{\;\simeq\;}\operatorname*{laxlim^{\dagger}}_{X\in\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{op}}}\mathcal{P}^{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{ad}}_{/X}),\qquad \Phi\mapsto\left((\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{ad}}_{/X})^{\mathrm{op}}\xrightarrow{\operatorname{fgt}}\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{op}}\xrightarrow{\Phi}\mathcal{C}\right)_{X\in\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{op}}},$$
where the morphisms in $\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{ad}}$ are marked.

**Theorem C (Corollary 5.6).** Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentably symmetric monoidal $\infty$-category. Then there is an equivalence of symmetric monoidal $\infty$-categories
$$\operatorname{Cond}(\mathcal{C})\simeq\operatorname*{laxlim^{\dagger}}_{X\in\operatorname{ExtrDisc}^{\mathrm{op}}}\operatorname{Sh}(X;\mathcal{C}),$$
where the open embeddings are marked.

**Theorem D (Theorem 6.5).** Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a compactly assembled presentable $\infty$-category. Then there is a preferred étale exodromy equivalence of $\infty$-categories
$$\operatorname{Sh}^{\mathrm{post}}_{\text{ét}}(\operatorname{Sch};\mathcal{C})\xrightarrow{\;\simeq\;}\operatorname{Fun}^{\mathrm{cts}}(\operatorname{GAL},\underline{\mathcal{C}}).$$
The same result holds over more general base schemes $T$ (Remark 6.6).
