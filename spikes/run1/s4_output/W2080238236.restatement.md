# Restatement

## Introduction

- The $N$-dimensional sphere $S^N$ and the real hyperbolic space $H^N$ are "dual" to each other as symmetric spaces [10]. Both are maximally symmetric.
- The eigenfunctions of the Laplacian for various fields on $S^N$ and $H^N$ can be computed explicitly.
- $S^4$ and $H^4$ are Euclidean sections of de Sitter and anti-de Sitter spacetimes. $S^3$ and $H^3$ appear as spatial sections of cosmological models.
- Earlier work by the same authors presented the eigenfunctions of the Laplacian for transverse-traceless totally-symmetric tensor fields [4] and for totally-antisymmetric tensor fields ($p$-forms) [5]. These fields are cross sections of homogeneous vector bundles.
- This paper studies spinor fields satisfying the Dirac equation $\not\!\nabla\psi = i\lambda\psi$ and the heat kernel for $\not\!\nabla^{2}$ on $S^N$ and $H^N$.
- Spinor fields on $S^N$ and $H^N$ are cross sections of the homogeneous vector bundles associated to the fundamental spinor representation(s) of $Spin(N)$.
- $S^N = Spin(N+1)/Spin(N)$ and $H^N = Spin(N,1)/Spin(N)$.
