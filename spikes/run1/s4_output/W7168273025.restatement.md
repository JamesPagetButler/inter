# Restatement

## Abstract (mathematical content)

- For a formal scheme $\mathfrak{X}$, the torsion subcategory $D_{\mathrm{tors}}(\mathfrak{X})$ is recovered as the maximal strongly compactly generated localizing tensor ideal of Efimov's category $\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(\mathfrak{X})$. The same holds for the Clausen–Scholze category $\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}(\mathfrak{X})$.
- Combining this with the Balmer spectrum, $\mathfrak{X}$ is reconstructed from the corresponding symmetric monoidal category of nuclear modules.
- For formal schemes topologically of finite type over a field or over $\mathbb{Z}$, the contravariant functor $\mathfrak{X}\mapsto\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(\mathfrak{X})$ is fully faithful. In the affine case, the analogous statement holds for $\mathfrak{X}\mapsto\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}(\mathfrak{X})$.

## 1. Introduction

**Notation.** For a formal scheme $\mathfrak{X}$, $\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(\mathfrak{X})$ denotes Efimov's category of nuclear modules [8]. $\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}(\mathfrak{X})$ denotes the Clausen–Scholze category of nuclear modules in the sense of [13, 1, 2]. For an $\infty$-category $C$, $\mathrm{Ho}(C)$ denotes its homotopy category.

**Reconstruction statements.** $D_{\mathrm{tors}}(\mathfrak{X})$ is recovered as a maximal strongly compactly generated localizing ideal. The formal scheme $\mathfrak{X}$ is then recovered from $D_{\mathrm{tors}}(\mathfrak{X})$ using a modification of Balmer's tensor triangular spectrum. A second method, in the spirit of Liu's functorial spectrum [9], uses field-valued points of the nuclear category.

**Notation for Theorem 1.2.** Let $k$ be a commutative ring.
- $\mathrm{fin.FSch}_k$ is the category of formal schemes topologically of finite type over $k$.
- $\mathrm{fin.aff.FSch}_k$ is the category of affine formal schemes topologically of finite type over $k$.
- $\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{dual}}_{\mathrm{st}}$ is the $\infty$-category of dualizable presentable stable $\infty$-categories and functors which admit two successive right adjoints.
- $\mathrm{CAlg}_k(\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{dual}}_{\mathrm{st}})$ is the $\infty$-category of commutative algebra objects in $\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{dual}}_{\mathrm{st}}$ under $D(k)$.

**Theorem 1.1 (Corollary 5.2).** Let $\mathfrak{X},\mathfrak{Y}$ be Noetherian formal schemes.
1. If there is a symmetric monoidal triangulated equivalence $\mathrm{Ho}(\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(\mathfrak{X}))\cong\mathrm{Ho}(\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(\mathfrak{Y}))$, then $\mathfrak{X}\cong\mathfrak{Y}$.
2. If $\mathfrak{X},\mathfrak{Y}$ are affine and there is a symmetric monoidal triangulated equivalence $\mathrm{Ho}(\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}(\mathfrak{X}))\cong\mathrm{Ho}(\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}(\mathfrak{Y}))$, then $\mathfrak{X}\cong\mathfrak{Y}$.

**Theorem 1.2.** Let $k=\mathbf{Z}$ or a field. The following functors are fully faithful:
$$(\mathrm{fin.FSch}_k)^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathrm{CAlg}_k(\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{dual}}_{\mathrm{st}}):\mathfrak{X}\mapsto\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(\mathfrak{X}),$$
$$(\mathrm{fin.aff.FSch}_k)^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathrm{CAlg}_k(\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{dual}}_{\mathrm{st}}):\mathfrak{X}\mapsto\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}(\mathfrak{X}).$$

## Assumptions and Notation

- A Grothendieck universe is assumed to exist.
- All rings are commutative and unital.
- All formal schemes are quasi-compact and quasi-separated, and admit a finitely generated ideal of definition locally.
- Unless otherwise specified, all triangulated categories admit small coproducts and are well generated.
- An $\infty$-category means an $(\infty,1)$-category. Ordinary categories and $(2,1)$-categories are regarded as $\infty$-categories in the usual way.
- For a functor $F$, its right adjoint, when it exists, is denoted $F^{\mathrm{R}}$.

## 2. Preliminaries

**Definition 2.1.** Let $T$ be a tt-category. A localizing ideal $I$ is called *strongly compactly generated* if it is compactly generated as a localizing subcategory.

**Lemma 2.2.** Let $T$ be a tt-category. There exists a unique maximal strongly compactly generated localizing ideal $I$.

**Definition 2.3.** For a tt-category $T$, the maximal strongly compactly generated localizing ideal of $T$ is denoted $T^{\mathrm{mcg}}$.

**Lemma 2.4.** Let $T$ be a tt-category such that $T^{\omega}\otimes T^{\omega}\subset T^{\omega}$, $1_T\in T^{\omega}$, and $T^{\omega}$ is rigid, that is, every object of $T^{\omega}$ is dualizable. Then every strongly compactly generated localizing ideal of $T$ is a smashing ideal.

**Definition 2.5 ([7, Definitions 8.5]).** Let $C$ be a compactly generated tt-$\infty$-category. An object $x\in C$ is called *nuclear* if, for every compact object $c\in C$, the natural map
$$\operatorname{RHom}(c,1_C)\otimes x\to\operatorname{RHom}(c,x)$$
is an isomorphism. The full subcategory of nuclear objects in $C$ is denoted $\mathrm{Nuc}(C)$.

**Definition 2.6.** Let $E$ be a locally rigid tt-$\infty$-category ([3, Definition C.1.1]) whose unit object $1_E$ is $\omega_1$-compact. Define
$$E^{\mathrm{rig}}=\mathrm{Nuc}(\mathrm{Ind}(E^{\omega_1})).$$

**Definition 2.7 (see [8]).** For a formal scheme $\mathfrak{X}$, define
$$\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(\mathfrak{X})=D_{\mathrm{tors}}(\mathfrak{X})^{\mathrm{rig}}.$$

**Proposition 2.8.** For formal schemes, the assignment $\mathfrak{X}\mapsto\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(\mathfrak{X})$ satisfies Zariski descent in $\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}}$. Moreover, for every open subset $U\subset\mathfrak{X}$, the restriction functor $\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(\mathfrak{X})\to\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(U)$ is a quotient functor, and its kernel is a smashing ideal.

**Definition 2.9 ([8, Proposition 7.5]).** For a Noetherian affine formal scheme $U=\mathrm{Spf}(R_I^{\wedge})$, define
$$\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}(U)=\mathrm{Nuc}\big(D((\mathrm{Proj}^{\omega_1,\mathrm{cpl}}_{R_I^{\wedge}})^{\mathrm{op}}\text{-}\mathrm{Mod})\big).$$
For a Noetherian formal scheme $\mathfrak{X}$, define $\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}(\mathfrak{X})$ by Zariski descent.

## 3. Torsion modules and nuclear categories

**Lemma 3.1.** Let $U=\mathrm{Spf}(R_I^{\wedge})$ be a Noetherian affine formal scheme, let $F\in D_{I\text{-}\mathrm{tors}}(R_I^{\wedge})$, and let $x\in\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(U)$. Then $\rho(F)\otimes x$ lies in the essential image of $D_{I\text{-}\mathrm{tors}}(R_I^{\wedge})$.

**Lemma 3.2.** Let $U=\mathrm{Spf}(R_I^{\wedge})$ be a Noetherian affine formal scheme, and let $F\in D(R_I^{\wedge})$. Assume that, for every $x\in\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}(U)$, the object $\rho(F)\otimes x$ lies in the essential image of $\rho$. Then $F\in D_{I\text{-}\mathrm{tors}}(R_I^{\wedge})$.

**Theorem 3.3.** Let $\mathfrak{X}$ be a Noetherian formal scheme. Then
$$\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(\mathfrak{X})^{\mathrm{mcg}}\cong D_{\mathrm{tors}}(\mathfrak{X}).$$
Moreover, $D_{\mathrm{tors}}(\mathfrak{X})$ is a strongly compactly generated localizing ideal of $\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}(\mathfrak{X})$. If there exists an affine open covering $\{U_i\}_i$ of $\mathfrak{X}$ such that each restriction functor $\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}(\mathfrak{X})\to\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}(U_i)$ is a quotient functor, then
$$\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}(\mathfrak{X})^{\mathrm{mcg}}\cong D_{\mathrm{tors}}(\mathfrak{X}).$$

**Lemma 3.4.** Let $U=\mathrm{Spf}(A_I^{\wedge})$ be a Noetherian affine formal scheme, and let $A\to B$ be a homomorphism of rings which factors through $A/I^m$ for some $m\geq 1$. Then
$$\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}(U)\otimes_{D(A_I^{\wedge})}D(B)\cong\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(U)\otimes_{D(A_I^{\wedge})}D(B)\cong D(B).$$

## 4. Modified Balmer spectrum

**Definition 4.1.** A tt-category $T$ is *weakly compactly generated* if it is compactly generated as a localizing subcategory of itself and $T^{\omega}\otimes T^{\omega}\subset T^{\omega}$. If moreover $1_T$ is compact, then $T$ is called a *compactly generated tt-category*.

**Definition 4.2.** Let $I\subset T$ be a compactly generated localizing ideal. $I$ is *prime* if $I\neq T$ and $a\otimes b\notin I$ for all $a,b\in T^{\omega}\setminus I^{\omega}$.

**Definition 4.3.** For a subset $U\subset\mathrm{Spc}(T)$, set
$$I_U=\langle a\in T^{\omega}\mid\mathrm{Supp}(a)\cap U=\emptyset\rangle,\qquad T_U=\mathrm{Idem}(T/I_U).$$
Here $\mathrm{Idem}$ denotes idempotent completion.

**Definition 4.4.** The ringed space $\mathrm{Spec}(T)=(\mathrm{Spc}(T),\mathcal{O}_T)$ is called the *Balmer spectrum* of a weakly compactly generated tt-category $T$.

**Remark 4.5.** If $T$ is a compactly generated tt-category, then this spectrum coincides with the usual Balmer spectrum of $T^{\omega}$.

**Definition 4.6.** Let $T,T'$ be weakly compactly generated tt-categories. A homomorphism $F:T\to T'$ of tt-categories is *cg-preserving* if, for every $a\in T^{\omega}$, the localizing ideal $\langle F(a)\rangle_{\otimes}$ is weakly finitely compactly generated.

**Proposition 4.7.** Let $F:T\to T'$ be a cg-preserving homomorphism of weakly compactly generated tt-categories. Then there is a natural morphism of ringed spaces
$$\mathrm{Spec}(T')\to\mathrm{Spec}(T):Q\mapsto\big\langle\{a\in T^{\omega}\mid F(a)\in Q\}\big\rangle.$$
Moreover, this construction defines a contravariant functor
$$\mathrm{Spec}:(\mathrm{tt}\text{-}\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{wcg}})^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathrm{LocSpaces}.$$

**Proposition 4.8.** Let $T$ be a weakly compactly generated tt-category, and let $I\subset T$ be a weakly finitely compactly generated smashing ideal. Then $I$ is a weakly compactly generated tt-category, and the right adjoint $\iota_I^{\mathrm{R}}:T\to I$ of the inclusion is a cg-preserving homomorphism. The map $\mathrm{Spc}(\iota_I^{\mathrm{R}}):\mathrm{Spc}(I)\to\mathrm{Spc}(T)$ is a closed embedding whose image is $\mathrm{Supp}(d)$, where $d\in I$ is a compact generator.

**Example 4.9 (cf. [5, Proposition 3.11]).** Let $T$ be a weakly compactly generated tt-category, and let $I\subset T$ be a compactly generated localizing ideal. Then $T/I$ is also a weakly compactly generated tt-category, and the quotient functor $L_I:T\to T/I$ is a cg-preserving homomorphism. The morphism $\mathrm{Spec}(L_I):\mathrm{Spec}(T/I)\to\mathrm{Spec}(T)$ is an embedding whose image is
$$\mathrm{Spc}(T)\setminus\bigcup_{a\in I^{\omega}}\mathrm{Supp}(a).$$

**Theorem 4.10.** Let $\mathfrak{X}$ be a quasi-compact quasi-separated formal scheme. Assume there exists an affine open cover $\{U_i\}$ such that each $U_i$ is isomorphic to $\mathrm{Spf}(R_I^{\wedge})$ for some ring $R$ and some ideal $I$ generated by a weakly proregular sequence (e.g. a Noetherian formal scheme). Then there is a natural isomorphism of ringed spaces
$$\mathrm{Spec}(\mathrm{Ho}(D_{\mathrm{tors}}(\mathfrak{X})))\cong\mathfrak{X}.$$

## 5. Reconstruction

**Theorem 5.1.** The compositions of the following functors are the canonical inclusions:
$$\mathrm{FSch}^{\mathrm{op}}\xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}\ }\mathrm{tt}\text{-}\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{mcg\text{-}fun}}_{\infty}\xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{Ho}\ }\mathrm{tt}\text{-}\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{mcg\text{-}fun}}\xrightarrow{\ (-)^{\mathrm{mcg}}\ }\mathrm{tt}\text{-}\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{wcg}}\xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{Spec}\ }\mathrm{LocSpaces}^{\mathrm{op}},$$
$$(\mathrm{FSch}^{\mathrm{qt}})^{\mathrm{op}}\xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}\ }\mathrm{tt}\text{-}\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{mcg\text{-}fun}}_{\infty}\xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{Ho}\ }\mathrm{tt}\text{-}\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{mcg\text{-}fun}}\xrightarrow{\ (-)^{\mathrm{mcg}}\ }\mathrm{tt}\text{-}\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{wcg}}\xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{Spec}\ }\mathrm{LocSpaces}^{\mathrm{op}}.$$

**Corollary 5.2.**
1. Let $\mathfrak{X},\mathfrak{Y}$ be Noetherian formal schemes. If there is a symmetric monoidal triangulated equivalence $\mathrm{Ho}(\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(\mathfrak{X}))\cong\mathrm{Ho}(\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(\mathfrak{Y}))$, then $\mathfrak{X}\cong\mathfrak{Y}$.
2. Let $\mathfrak{X},\mathfrak{Y}\in\mathrm{FSch}^{\mathrm{qt}}$. If there is a symmetric monoidal triangulated equivalence $\mathrm{Ho}(\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}(\mathfrak{X}))\cong\mathrm{Ho}(\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}(\mathfrak{Y}))$, then $\mathfrak{X}\cong\mathfrak{Y}$.

**Corollary 5.3.**
1. The functor $\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}:\mathrm{FSch}^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathrm{tt}\text{-}\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{mcg\text{-}fun}}_{\infty}$ is fully faithful.
2. For any $\mathfrak{X}\in\mathrm{FSch}^{\mathrm{qt}}$ and $\mathfrak{Y}\in\mathrm{FSch}$, there is a natural equivalence
$$\operatorname{Map}_{\mathrm{tt}\text{-}\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{mcg\text{-}fun}}_{\infty}}\big(\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}(\mathfrak{X}),\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(\mathfrak{Y})\big)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(\mathfrak{Y},\mathfrak{X}).$$
3. The functor $\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}:(\mathrm{FSch}^{\mathrm{qt}})^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathrm{tt}\text{-}\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{mcg\text{-}fun}}_{\infty}$ is fully faithful.

## 6. Full faithfulness via field-valued points

**Definition 6.1.** Let $k$ be a ring and $\mathfrak{X}$ a $k$-formal scheme. $\mathfrak{X}$ is *nearly of $<\mathfrak{c}$-type* if, for every point $x\in\mathfrak{X}$, $\mathrm{tr.deg}_k(\kappa(x))<\mathfrak{c}$.

**Lemma 6.2.** Let $k$ be $\mathbf{Z}$, or a field. Let $R$ be a $k$-algebra, $I\subset R$ an ideal, and $\mathfrak{p}\subset R_I^{\wedge}$ a prime ideal. If $\mathrm{tr.deg}_k\,\kappa(\mathfrak{p})<\mathfrak{c}$, then $I\subset\mathfrak{p}$.

**Lemma 6.3.** Let $D,E\in\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}})$, and let $I\subset D$ be a smashing ideal. Assume that, for $x,y\in E$, $x\otimes y\cong 0$ implies $x\cong 0$ or $y\cong 0$. Then
$$\operatorname{Map}_{\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}})}(D,E)\cong\operatorname{Map}_{\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}})}(D/I,E)\amalg\operatorname{Map}_{\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}})}(I,E).$$

**Lemma 6.4.** Let $D,E$ be as in Lemma 6.3. Let $I_1,I_2\subset D$ be smashing ideals, let $I_3=\langle I_1,I_2\rangle_{\otimes}$, and let $e_i\to 1_D$ be the corresponding left idempotents for $i=1,2,3$. Assume $e_1\otimes e_2\cong 0$. Then the following diagram is coCartesian, with corners (in the printed order)
$$\operatorname{Map}_{\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}})}(D/I_3,E),\quad \operatorname{Map}_{\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}})}(D/I_2,E),\quad \operatorname{Map}_{\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}})}(D/I_1,E),\quad \operatorname{Map}_{\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}})}(D,E).$$

**Theorem 6.5.** Let $k$ be $\mathbf{Z}$ or a field, and let $\mathfrak{X}$ be a Noetherian formal scheme over $k$. Then the map of sets
$$\phi:\{x\in\mathfrak{X}\mid\mathrm{tr.deg}_k(\kappa(x))<\mathfrak{c}\}\to\pi_0\big((\mathrm{Fld}_k)_{\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(\mathfrak{X})/}\big),$$
$$x\mapsto\big(\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(\mathfrak{X})\to\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(\kappa(x))\cong D(\kappa(x))\big),$$
is bijective. If $\mathfrak{X}\in\mathrm{FSch}^{\mathrm{sq}}_k$, the same assertion holds for $\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}(\mathfrak{X})$.

**Corollary 6.6.** Let $k$ be $\mathbf{Z}$ or a field, and let $\mathfrak{X}$ be a Noetherian formal scheme over $k$. Then
$$D_{\mathrm{tors}}(\mathfrak{X})^{\perp}\subset\bigcap_{F\in(\mathrm{Fld}_k)_{\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(\mathfrak{X})/}}\mathrm{Ker}(F).$$
If $\mathfrak{X}$ is nearly of $<\mathfrak{c}$-type over $k$, then equality holds. If $\mathfrak{X}\in\mathrm{FSch}^{\mathrm{sq}}_k$, the same assertion holds for $\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}(\mathfrak{X})$.

**Theorem 6.7.** Let $k$ be $\mathbf{Z}$ or a field, and let $\mathfrak{X},\mathfrak{Y}$ be Noetherian formal schemes over $k$. Assume that $\mathfrak{Y}$ is nearly of $<\mathfrak{c}$-type over $k$. Then
$$\operatorname{Map}_{\mathrm{CAlg}_k(\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{dual}}_{\mathrm{st}})}\big(\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(\mathfrak{X}),\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(\mathfrak{Y})\big)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(\mathfrak{Y},\mathfrak{X}).$$
Moreover, if $\mathfrak{X}\in\mathrm{FSch}^{\mathrm{sq}}_k$, then
$$\operatorname{Map}_{\mathrm{CAlg}_k(\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{dual}}_{\mathrm{st}})}\big(\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}(\mathfrak{X}),\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{Ef}}(\mathfrak{Y})\big)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(\mathfrak{Y},\mathfrak{X}).$$
If $\mathfrak{X},\mathfrak{Y}\in\mathrm{FSch}^{\mathrm{sq}}_k$, then
$$\operatorname{Map}_{\mathrm{CAlg}_k(\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{dual}}_{\mathrm{st}})}\big(\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}(\mathfrak{X}),\mathrm{Nuc}^{\mathrm{CS}}(\mathfrak{Y})\big)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(\mathfrak{Y},\mathfrak{X}).$$

## Appendix A

**Lemma A.1.** Let $F:C\to D$ be a homomorphism of tt-$\infty$-categories. Assume that $F$ is essentially surjective and that there exists a right idempotent $\gamma:1_C\to f$ in $C$ such that $F^{\mathrm{R}}F(x)\cong x\otimes f$ naturally in $x\in C$, and such that, under this equivalence, the unit map $x\to F^{\mathrm{R}}F(x)$ is identified with $x\otimes\gamma:x\to x\otimes f$. Then $F$ is a quotient functor, and its kernel is a smashing ideal.

**Lemma A.2.** Let $C$ be a tt-$\infty$-category, and let $I$ be a smashing ideal in $C$. Then, for any $x,y\in C$, there is a natural isomorphism
$$\operatorname{RHom}_I(\iota_I^{\mathrm{R}}(x),\iota_I^{\mathrm{R}}(y))\cong\iota_I^{\mathrm{R}}\operatorname{RHom}_C(x,y).$$

**Proposition A.3.** Let $C$ be a small symmetric monoidal stable $\infty$-category, let $\gamma:1_C\to f$ be a right idempotent in $C$, let $I=\{x\in C\mid x\otimes f\cong 0\}$ be the corresponding thick ideal, let $L:C\to C/I$ be the quotient functor, and let
$$\mathrm{Nuc}(\mathrm{Ind}(L)):\mathrm{Nuc}(\mathrm{Ind}(C))\to\mathrm{Nuc}(\mathrm{Ind}(C/I))$$
be the induced functor. Assume that, for every $x\in C$, the object $\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathrm{Ind}(C^{\mathrm{op}})}(x^{\mathrm{op}},1)$ is nuclear in $\mathrm{Ind}(C^{\mathrm{op}})$. Then $\mathrm{Nuc}(\mathrm{Ind}(L))$ is a quotient functor, and its kernel is a smashing ideal. The corresponding right idempotent is $\nu_C(f)$, where $\nu_C$ is the right adjoint of the inclusion $\mathrm{Nuc}(\mathrm{Ind}(C))\to\mathrm{Ind}(C)$.

**Corollary A.4.** Let $E$ be a locally rigid symmetric monoidal stable $\infty$-category with $\omega_1$-compact unit object $1_E$, and let $I\subset E$ be a smashing ideal with corresponding right idempotent $\gamma:1_E\to f$. Assume that $f$ is $\omega_1$-compact. Then $E^{\mathrm{rig}}\to(E/I)^{\mathrm{rig}}$ is a quotient functor, and its kernel is a smashing ideal. The corresponding right idempotent is $\Lambda_E(\gamma)$.

**Proposition A.5.** Let $E$ be a locally rigid symmetric monoidal stable $\infty$-category with $\omega_1$-compact unit object $1_E$. Let $I_1,I_2\subset E$ be smashing ideals with corresponding right idempotents $\gamma_1:1_E\to f_1$ and $\gamma_2:1_E\to f_2$. Assume that $f_1$ and $f_2$ are $\omega_1$-compact and that $I_1\cap I_2=0$. Let $I_3=\langle I_1,I_2\rangle_{\otimes}$. Then the following diagram is Cartesian in $\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}}$, with corners (in the printed order)
$$E^{\mathrm{rig}},\quad(E/I_2)^{\mathrm{rig}},\quad(E/I_1)^{\mathrm{rig}},\quad(E/I_3)^{\mathrm{rig}}.$$
