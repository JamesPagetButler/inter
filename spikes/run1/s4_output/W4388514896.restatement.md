# Restatement

## Abstract

We show that the nearby cycles functor for the $p$-adic Hecke stack at parahoric level is perverse t-exact. This is done by developing a theory of Wakimoto filtrations at Iwahori level. The functor also lifts to the $\mathbb{E}_1$-center. Using these tools, we construct the Arkhipov–Bezrukavnikov functor for $p$-adic affine flag varieties at Iwahori level. We prove that it is an equivalence for all classical groups and for the exceptional groups of types $E_6$ and $E_7$.

## 1. Introduction

Let $F$ be a $p$-adic field with ring of integers $O$ and residue field $k$. Let $G$ be a connected reductive $F$-group with a parahoric $O$-model $\mathcal{G}$. The paper has two goals:

1. Define a $p$-adic analogue of the Gaitsgory central functor [Gai01]. It sends perverse sheaves on $\operatorname{Hk}_{G,C}$, where $C$ is the completed algebraic closure of $F$, to central perverse sheaves on $\operatorname{Hk}_{\mathcal{G},\bar{k}}$.
2. When $\mathcal{G}=\mathcal{I}$ is Iwahori, define a $p$-adic analogue of the Arkhipov–Bezrukavnikov functor [AB09]. It relates coherent sheaves on the dual Springer variety $\hat{\mathcal{N}}_{\mathrm{Spr}}$ to constructible étale sheaves on $\operatorname{Hk}_{\mathcal{I},\bar{k}}$.

### 1.1. Hecke algebras

Let $G$ be a pinned split connected reductive $F$-group, with Borel $B$, maximal split torus $T\subset B\subset G$, and pinning. The Iwahori–Weyl group is $W=N(F)/T(O)$, where $N$ is the normalizer of $T$. It carries a length function $\ell$ making $(W,\ell)$ a quasi-Coxeter group.

The affine Hecke algebra $\mathbb{H}$ is the $\mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1/2}]$-algebra with basis $\{T_w\mid w\in W\}$ subject to:
- $T_wT_{w'}=T_{ww'}$ if $\ell(w)+\ell(w')=\ell(ww')$;
- $(T_s+q^{-1/2})(T_s-q^{1/2})=0$ for all length-$1$ elements $s$.

This is the Iwahori–Matsumoto presentation [IM65]. Let $I:=\mathcal{I}(O)$. The Iwahori–Hecke algebra is $\mathcal{H}:=C_c(I\backslash G(F)/I)$, with convolution, where $G(F)$ carries the Haar measure giving $I$ measure $1$. There is an isomorphism
$$\mathbb{H}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]}\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell\simeq\mathcal{H},$$
where $q$ maps to the cardinality of $k$.

Let $\mathbb{H}_{\mathrm{f}}\subset\mathbb{H}$ be the finite Hecke algebra. The anti-spherical module is
$$\mathbb{M}^{\mathrm{as}}:=\mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1/2}]^{\mathrm{sgn}}\otimes_{\mathbb{H}_{\mathrm{f}}}\mathbb{H},$$
where $T_w\in\mathbb{H}_{\mathrm{f}}$ acts on $\mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1/2}]^{\mathrm{sgn}}$ by multiplication by $(-1)^{\ell(w)}q^{1/2}$. Set $\mathcal{M}^{\mathrm{as}}:=\mathbb{M}^{\mathrm{as}}\otimes\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$.

Let $\hat{G}$ be the Langlands dual group, and $\hat U\subset\hat B\subset\hat G$ a Borel and its unipotent radical. The Springer resolution is
$$p_{\mathrm{Spr}}:\hat{\mathcal{N}}_{\mathrm{Spr}}=\hat G\times^{\hat B}\mathrm{Lie}\,\hat U\to\hat{\mathcal{N}}.$$
The Steinberg variety is $\hat{\mathrm{St}}:=\hat{\mathcal{N}}_{\mathrm{Spr}}\times_{\hat{\mathcal{N}}}\hat{\mathcal{N}}_{\mathrm{Spr}}$.

Kazhdan–Lusztig [KL87] showed that $\mathbb{H}\cong K_0([\hat G\times\mathbb{G}_m\backslash\hat{\mathrm{St}}])$, and $\mathbb{M}^{\mathrm{as}}\cong K_0([\hat G\times\mathbb{G}_m\backslash\hat{\mathcal{N}}_{\mathrm{Spr}}])$.

Iwahori–Matsumoto [IM65] also gives $\mathcal{H}\cong K_0(\mathscr{P}(\operatorname{Hk}_{\mathcal{I}}))$, where $\operatorname{Hk}_{\mathcal{I}}=L^+\mathcal{I}\backslash LG/L^+\mathcal{I}$.

The action of $\mathcal{H}$ on $\mathcal{M}^{\mathrm{as}}$ gives a surjection $\mathcal{H}\to\mathcal{M}^{\mathrm{as}}$. Its kernel is generated by the Kazhdan–Lusztig basis elements indexed by those $w\in W$ that are not minimal in their left $W_{\mathrm{fin}}$-coset.

### 1.2. The central functor

The paper uses the Fargues–Scholze geometric Satake equivalence $\mathrm{Sat}$ [FS21]. It defines $\mathscr{Z}(V)=R\Psi(\mathrm{Sat}(V))$ for representations $V$ of $\hat G$. The following is the main result on $\mathscr{Z}$.

**Theorem 1.1 (Theorems 4.15 and 4.17, Corollaries 4.20 and 4.21).** The functor
$$\mathscr{Z}\colon\mathrm{Rep}(\hat G)\to\mathscr{D}_{\mathrm{ula}}(\operatorname{Hk}_{\mathcal{G},k})$$
lifts to an $\mathbb{E}_2$-monoidal functor towards the $\mathbb{E}_1$-center of the right side. Moreover, each $\mathscr{Z}(V)$ is perverse and has unipotent monodromy. If $\mathcal{G}=\mathcal{I}$ is Iwahori, then $\mathscr{Z}(V)$ is convolution-exact and admits a Wakimoto filtration whose associated graded equals $\mathscr{I}(V_{|\hat T})$.

The Wakimoto functor $\mathscr{I}\colon\mathrm{Rep}(\hat T)\to\mathscr{P}(\operatorname{Hk}_{\mathcal{I}})$ is defined at the level of complexes, following [AB09] and [AR].

### 1.3. The AB functor and Iwahori–Whittaker sheaves

Let $[\hat G\backslash\hat{\mathcal{N}}_{\mathrm{Spr}}]$ be the Springer stack.

**Theorem 1.2 (Proposition 5.7).** There is a monoidal functor
$$\mathscr{F}\colon\mathrm{Perf}([\hat G\backslash\hat{\mathcal{N}}_{\mathrm{Spr}}])\to\mathscr{D}_{\mathrm{cons}}(\operatorname{Hk}_{\mathcal{I}})$$
extending $\mathscr{Z}\times\mathscr{I}$.

The category $\mathscr{D}_{\mathrm{cons}}(\operatorname{Hk}_{\mathcal{I}\mathcal{W}})$ consists of $(L^+\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{u}}^{\mathrm{op}},\mathcal{L})$-twisted equivariant constructible sheaves on $\mathrm{Fl}_{\mathcal{I}}$, where $\mathcal{L}$ is an Artin–Schreier character sheaf. Its perverse category $\mathscr{P}(\operatorname{Hk}_{\mathcal{I}\mathcal{W}})$ is a highest weight category in the sense of [BGS96], with simple and tilting objects indexed by $\mathbb{X}_*(T)$.

The averaging functor $\mathrm{av}_{\mathcal{I}\mathcal{W}}\colon\mathscr{D}_{\mathrm{cons}}(\operatorname{Hk}_{\mathcal{I}})\to\mathscr{D}_{\mathrm{cons}}(\operatorname{Hk}_{\mathcal{I}\mathcal{W}})$ is perverse t-exact. It is given by left convolution against the simple object attached to $0\in\mathbb{X}_*(T)$. Define $\mathscr{Z}_{\mathcal{I}\mathcal{W}}:=\mathrm{av}_{\mathcal{I}\mathcal{W}}\circ\mathscr{Z}$ and $\mathscr{F}_{\mathcal{I}\mathcal{W}}:=\mathrm{av}_{\mathcal{I}\mathcal{W}}\circ\mathscr{F}$.

**Theorem 1.3 (Theorem 7.9).** If $G$ has enough minuscules (Definition 7.8), then $\mathscr{Z}_{\mathcal{I}\mathcal{W}}(V)$ is tilting.

**Theorem 1.4 (Proposition 8.3, Proposition 10.8).** If $G$ has enough minuscules, there is a symmetric monoidal equivalence
$$\mathscr{P}_0(\operatorname{Hk}_{\mathcal{I}})\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathrm{Rep}(Z_{\hat G}(n_0)),$$
where $n_0$ is a regular nilpotent element. Here $\mathscr{P}_0(\operatorname{Hk}_{\mathcal{I}})$ is the Serre quotient of perverse sheaves obtained by modding out IC sheaves with positive-dimensional support.

**Theorem 1.5 (Theorem 9.1).** If $G$ has enough minuscules, then
$$\mathscr{F}_{\mathcal{I}\mathcal{W}}\colon\mathrm{Perf}([\hat G\backslash\hat{\mathcal{N}}_{\mathrm{Spr}}])\to\mathscr{D}_{\mathrm{cons}}(\operatorname{Hk}_{\mathcal{I}\mathcal{W}})$$
is an equivalence.

**Corollary 9.2 (as stated in the introduction).** The anti-spherical Serre quotient $\mathscr{P}_{\mathrm{as}}(\operatorname{Hk}_{\mathcal{I}})$ is identified with the category $\mathscr{P}(\operatorname{Hk}_{\mathcal{I}\mathcal{W}})$ of Iwahori–Whittaker perverse sheaves.

## 2. Schubert varieties

**Lemma 2.1.** The following are equivalent:
1. The $\Gamma$-action on $W$ is trivial.
2. $\Gamma$ acts trivially on $\bar{\mathbb{X}}_\bullet$.
3. $G$ is residually split, i.e. the reductive quotient $\mathcal{G}_k^{\mathrm{red}}$ of the special fiber of every parahoric $O$-model of $G$ is split over $k$.

**Lemma 2.2.**
1. The map $N(\breve F)\to\mathrm{Fl}_{\mathcal{G}}$, $n\mapsto n\cdot L^+\mathcal{G}$, induces a bijection on underlying topological spaces
$$W/W_{\mathbf{f}}=\mathcal{T}(\breve O)\backslash N(\breve F)/(N(\breve F)\cap\mathcal{G}(\breve O))\to\operatorname{Hk}_{(\mathcal{I},\mathcal{G})}.$$
So the $L^+\mathcal{I}$-orbits $\mathrm{Fl}_{(\mathcal{I},\mathcal{G}),w}:=L^+\mathcal{I}\cdot w\subset\mathrm{Fl}_{\mathcal{G}}$ are indexed by $W/W_{\mathbf{f}}$.
2. The $L^+\mathcal{I}$-orbits on $\mathrm{Fl}_{\mathcal{G}}$ form a stratification. The closure $\mathrm{Fl}_{(\mathcal{I},\mathcal{G}),\leq w}$ is the unique closed perfect subscheme with
$$\bigl|\mathrm{Fl}_{(\mathcal{I},\mathcal{G}),\leq w}\bigr|=\bigcup_{w'\leq w}\bigl|\mathrm{Fl}_{(\mathcal{I},\mathcal{G}),w'}\bigr|$$
for the Bruhat order on $W$.

**Definition 2.3.** The perfect projective schemes $\mathrm{Fl}_{(\mathcal{I},\mathcal{G}),\leq w}$ are called *Schubert varieties*. Their open dense subschemes $\mathrm{Fl}_{(\mathcal{I},\mathcal{G}),w}$ are called *(Iwahori) Schubert cells*.

**Lemma 2.4.** Let $w_1,\ldots,w_n\in W$, and assume $w_1\cdots w_n$ is right $W_{\mathbf{f}}$-minimal and reduced.
1. The map $\mathrm{Fl}_{\mathcal{I},w_1}\tilde\times\cdots\tilde\times\mathrm{Fl}_{\mathcal{I},w_n}\to\mathrm{Fl}_{\mathcal{G}}$ has image in $\mathrm{Fl}_{(\mathcal{I},\mathcal{G}),w_1\cdots w_n}$ and induces an isomorphism
$$\mathrm{Fl}_{\mathcal{I},w_1}\tilde\times\cdots\tilde\times\mathrm{Fl}_{\mathcal{I},w_n}\cong\mathrm{Fl}_{(\mathcal{I},\mathcal{G}),w_1\cdots w_n}.$$
2. We have $m(\mathrm{Fl}_{\mathcal{I},\leq w_1}\tilde\times\cdots\tilde\times\mathrm{Fl}_{\mathcal{I},\leq w_n})\subseteq\mathrm{Fl}_{(\mathcal{I},\mathcal{G}),\leq w_1\cdots w_n}$, and the map
$$\mathrm{Fl}_{\mathcal{I},\leq w_1}\tilde\times\cdots\tilde\times\mathrm{Fl}_{\mathcal{I},\leq w_n}\to\mathrm{Fl}_{(\mathcal{I},\mathcal{G}),\leq w_1\cdots w_n}$$
is (perfectly) proper and birational.
3. If $w\in W$ and $\tau\in\Omega_{\mathbf{a}}$, then $\mathrm{Fl}_{(\mathcal{I},\mathcal{G}),w}\to\mathrm{Fl}_{(\mathcal{I},\mathcal{G}),\tau w}$, $gL^+\mathcal{G}\mapsto\tau gL^+\mathcal{G}$, is an isomorphism.

**Lemma 2.5.** Let $w\in W/W_{\mathbf{f}}$, and let $w_{\min}\in W$ be its right $W_{\mathbf{f}}$-minimal representative.
1. $\mathrm{Fl}_{(\breve{\mathcal{I}},\breve{\mathcal{G}}),w}\cong\mathbb{A}^{\ell(w_{\min}),\mathrm{pf}}_k$. In particular $\dim\mathrm{Fl}_{(\mathcal{I},\mathcal{G}),w}=\ell(w_{\min})$.
2. If $\ell(w_{\min})=0$, i.e. $w_{\min}=\tau\in\Omega_{\mathbf{a}}$, then $\mathrm{Fl}_{(\mathcal{I},\mathcal{G}),w}=\mathrm{Fl}_{(\mathcal{I},\mathcal{G}),\leq w}\cong\operatorname{Spec}(k)$.
3. If $\ell(w_{\min})=1$, i.e. $w_{\min}=\tau s$ with $s$ a simple reflection and $\tau\in\Omega_{\mathbf{a}}$, then
$$\mathrm{Fl}_{(\mathcal{I},\mathcal{G}),\leq w}\cong\mathbb{P}^{1,\mathrm{pf}}_k.$$
