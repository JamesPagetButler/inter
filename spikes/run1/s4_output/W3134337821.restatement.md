## Abstract (restated)

Following the idea of [Far16], the authors develop the foundations of the geometric Langlands program on the Fargues–Fontaine curve. They:

- define a category of $\ell$-adic sheaves on the stack $\mathrm{Bun}_G$ of $G$-bundles on the Fargues–Fontaine curve;
- prove a geometric Satake equivalence over the Fargues–Fontaine curve;
- study the stack of $L$-parameters.

As applications, they:

- prove finiteness results for the cohomology of local Shimura varieties and of general moduli spaces of local shtukas;
- define $L$-parameters associated with irreducible smooth representations of $G(E)$;
- define a map from the spectral Bernstein center to the Bernstein center;
- define the spectral action of the category of perfect complexes on the stack of $L$-parameters on the category of $\ell$-adic sheaves on $\mathrm{Bun}_G$.
