# Restatement

## Abstract and Introduction

The article discusses how abstraction boundaries can help manage complexity in mathematical research with the help of an interactive theorem prover. It argues that the use of an interactive theorem prover gives additional qualitative benefits in implementing these ideas, which mathematicians have used implicitly for some time.

The introduction makes the following points.

- Modern pure mathematics has a clear tendency toward increasing complexity. Complex proof techniques, deep subfield mastery, and input from several areas all contribute to this. Papers of one hundred pages or more are routine, and refereeing a complex result regularly takes multiple years.
- Mathematicians tame complexity by using abstraction: breaking proofs into lemmas and propositions, introducing definitions that capture selected properties, and creating "abstraction boundaries" that separate the use of a mathematical object from its implementation.
- The article discusses how abstraction boundaries, used together with an interactive theorem prover (ITP), can facilitate complex interdisciplinary collaboration. The implementation of these ideas in an ITP is argued to reduce cognitive load for authors and readers. The liquid tensor experiment (LTE) serves as the main example.

The outline of the paper is as follows.

- §2 reviews aspects of condensed mathematics and LTE.
- §3 describes abstraction boundaries and sources of complexity in mathematics.
- §4 highlights qualitative benefits of abstraction boundaries alongside an ITP, illustrated by spec driven development with examples from LTE.
- §5 addresses aligning formal and informal mathematics using abductive reasoning.
- §6 summarises and concludes.

## Theorem A (Clausen–Scholze)

Let $0 < p' < p \leq 1$ be real numbers, and let $S$ be a profinite set. Let $V$ be a $p$-Banach space, considered as a condensed abelian group. Write $\mathcal{M}_{p'}(S)$ for the space of real-valued $p'$-measures on $S$, also considered as a condensed abelian group. Then

$$\operatorname{Ext}^{i}_{\operatorname{Cond}(\mathrm{Ab})}\big(\mathcal{M}_{p'}(S),\, V\big) = 0 \quad \text{for all } i \geq 1.$$
