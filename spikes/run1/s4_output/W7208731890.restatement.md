# Restatement

## Setting and questions

Throughout, $\mathcal{V}\in\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{L})$ denotes a presentably symmetric monoidal $\infty$-category.

**Question 1.1.** Is it possible to formulate the telescope conjecture for an arbitrary big $tt$-category, or more generally, for any presentably symmetric monoidal $\infty$-category $\mathcal{V}\in\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{L})$?

**Question 1.2.** Does there exist a natural extension of the Balmer frame to an arbitrary $\mathcal{V}\in\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{L})$ such that:
1. it depends functorially on $\mathcal{V}$, inducing a functor $\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{L})\to\mathsf{Frm}$;
2. it admits a natural embedding into the smashing frame of $\mathcal{V}$;
3. it recovers the classical Balmer frame when $\mathcal{V}$ is stable and compactly-rigidly generated?

## Smashing frames and related adjunctions

**Definition 1.3.** Let $\mathcal{V}\in\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{L})$. The smashing frame of $\mathcal{V}$ is the poset $\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{V})$ of coidempotent objects.

Aoki establishes the sheaves–spectrum adjunction [4]:
$$\mathsf{Frm}\rightleftarrows \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{L}),\qquad \mathrm{Shv}(-)\dashv \mathrm{cIdem}(-).$$

**Proposition 1.4 (Proposition 4.1).** Let $\mathcal{V}\in\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{L})$. The image of the canonical embedding
$$\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{\mathrm{at}}_{\mathcal{V}})\hookrightarrow\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{\mathrm{dbl}}_{\mathcal{V}})$$
can be identified with the $\mathcal{V}$-atomically generated smashing ideals of $\mathcal{V}$. In particular, the subposet of atomically generated smashing ideals forms a subframe.

**Definition 1.5.** Let $\mathcal{V}\in\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{L})$. A smashing ideal $\mathcal{I}\hookrightarrow\mathcal{V}$ is called an *atomic smashing ideal* if it is generated, as a $\mathcal{V}$-submodule, by $\mathcal{V}$-atomic objects (equivalently, by dualizable objects). The *atomic smashing frame* of $\mathcal{V}$ is the subposet
$$\mathrm{cIdem}_{f}(\mathcal{V})\subset\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{V})$$
consisting of those coidempotent objects $x\to\mathbf{1}$ whose associated smashing ideal $\langle x\rangle\hookrightarrow\mathcal{V}$ is atomic.

The source displays the following commutative square, in which the horizontal maps are $\sim$ and the vertical maps are $\langle-\rangle$:
$$\begin{array}{ccc}
\mathrm{cIdem}_{f}(\mathcal{V}) & \hookrightarrow & \mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{V})\\
\downarrow{\scriptstyle\sim} & & \downarrow{\scriptstyle\sim}\\
\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{\mathrm{at}}_{\mathcal{V}}) & \hookrightarrow & \mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{\mathrm{dbl}}_{\mathcal{V}})
\end{array}$$

**Proposition 1.6 (Corollary 4.20).** Let $\mathcal{V}\in\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{L})$, and let $\mathcal{W}\in\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{L}_{\mathcal{V}})$ be a locally rigid algebra over $\mathcal{V}$ such that $\mathcal{W}$ is $\mathcal{V}$-atomically generated. A smashing ideal $\mathcal{I}\hookrightarrow\mathcal{W}$ is an atomic smashing ideal of $\mathcal{W}$ if and only if it is $\mathcal{V}$-atomically generated when regarded as a $\mathcal{V}$-module.

**Example 1.7.**
1. Suppose $\mathcal{C}\in\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{L}_{\mathrm{st}})$ is compactly generated and locally rigid over $\mathrm{Sp}$ (e.g. $\mathrm{Sp}$, $L^{f}_{n}\mathrm{Sp}$, $L_{n}\mathrm{Sp}$, $\mathrm{Sp}_{T(n)}$, $\mathrm{Sp}_{K(n)}$, and $\mathrm{Sp}_{p\text{-}\mathrm{cpl}}$). Then a smashing ideal $\mathcal{I}\hookrightarrow\mathcal{C}$ is atomic if and only if it is compactly generated.
2. Suppose $\mathcal{C}\in\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{L}_{\mathrm{ad}})$ is compact projectively generated and locally rigid over $\mathrm{Sp}_{\geq 0}$ (e.g. $\mathrm{Sp}_{\geq 0}$, $\mathrm{Sp}_{G,\geq 0}$, $\mathrm{Fil}(\mathrm{Sp})_{h\geq 0}$, $\mathrm{Syn}_{R,\geq 0}$, and $\mathrm{DM}(k,\mathbf{Z}[1/p])_{c\geq 0}$; see Example 7.4). Then a smashing ideal $\mathcal{I}\hookrightarrow\mathcal{C}$ is atomic if and only if it is compact projectively generated.

**Theorem A (Theorems 4.26, 4.32).** Let $\mathcal{V}\in\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{L})$.
1. If the unit of $\mathcal{V}$ is compact, then $\mathrm{cIdem}_{f}(\mathcal{V})$ is a coherent frame.
2. If the compact dualizable objects of $\mathcal{V}$ generate $\mathcal{V}$ as a localizing ideal, then $\mathrm{cIdem}_{f}(\mathcal{V})$ is a locally coherent frame.

In both cases, $\mathrm{cIdem}_{f}(\mathcal{V})$ is spatial.

**Definition 1.8.** Let $\mathcal{V}\in\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{L})$. The *telescope conjecture for $\mathcal{V}$* holds if the canonical inclusion $\mathrm{cIdem}_{f}(\mathcal{V})\hookrightarrow\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{V})$ is an equivalence, equivalently, if every smashing ideal of $\mathcal{V}$ is an atomic smashing ideal.

**Theorem B (Theorems 4.43, 4.46, 5.21).** Let $\mathcal{V}\in\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{L})$, and let $\{f_{i}\colon\mathcal{V}\to\mathcal{V}_{i}\}$ be one of the following:
1. an atomic Hopf covering of $\mathcal{V}$;
2. an atomic smashing-open covering of $\mathcal{V}$;
3. a finite atomic smashing-closed covering of $\mathcal{V}$, provided that $\mathcal{V}$ is compactly-rigidly generated and stable.

Then $\mathcal{V}$ satisfies the telescope conjecture whenever each $\mathcal{V}_{i}$ does. In cases (2) and (3), this condition is also necessary.

## Recollement for (atomic) smashing frames

The identification of smashing ideals with locally rigid localizations is given by (see Theorem 3.28):
$$\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{V})\xrightarrow{\sim}\{\text{locally rigid localizations of }\mathcal{V}\}.$$

**Theorem C (Theorems 5.7, 5.17).** Let $\mathcal{C}\in\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{L})$ be prestable, and let
$$\mathcal{I}\xhookrightarrow{i}\mathcal{C}\xrightarrow{L}\mathcal{C}/\mathcal{I}$$
be a smashing localization sequence. Then:

1. There are natural equivalences
$$\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{I})\simeq\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{C})_{\leq\mathcal{I}},\qquad \mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{C}/\mathcal{I})\simeq\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{C})_{\geq\mathcal{I}}.$$
Consequently, the canonical morphism
$$\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{C})\xrightarrow{(i^{R},L)}\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{I})\times\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{C}/\mathcal{I})$$
is an embedding of frames. Moreover, $\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{C})$ is obtained by Artin gluing:
$$\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{C})\simeq\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{I})\overleftarrow{\times}_{\phi_{\mathcal{I}}}\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{C}/\mathcal{I}),$$
where the gluing functor is
$$\phi_{\mathcal{I}}(a)=\mathcal{I}\vee(\mathcal{I}\backslash a),\qquad a\in\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{C})_{\leq\mathcal{I}}.$$
Here $\mathcal{I}\backslash a$ denotes the Heyting implication in the frame $\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{C})$.

2. If $\mathcal{C}$ is stable and compactly-rigidly generated, and $\mathcal{I}$ is an atomic smashing ideal, then the assertions of (1) hold with smashing frames replaced by atomic smashing frames.

**Corollary 1.9 (Corollary 5.12).** Let $\mathcal{C}\in\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{L})$ be prestable, and let $\mathcal{I}\xhookrightarrow{i}\mathcal{C}\xrightarrow{L}\mathcal{C}/\mathcal{I}$ be a smashing localization. Then $\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{C})$ is spatial if and only if both $\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{I})$ and $\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{C}/\mathcal{I})$ are spatial.

**Theorem D (Theorem 5.15).** Let $\mathcal{C}\in\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{L}_{\mathrm{ad}})$ be prestable. Then
$$\mathrm{Z}\bigl(\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{C})\bigr)\subset\mathrm{cIdem}_{f}(\mathcal{C}).$$

## The smashing frame of $\mathrm{Sp}$

**Theorem E (Theorems 6.6, 6.14).**
1. Both the smashing frame and the atomic smashing frame of $\mathrm{Sp}$ decompose into those of $\mathrm{Sp}_{(p)}$. Specifically, the horizontal arrows in the following diagram of frames are equivalences:
$$\begin{array}{ccc}
\mathrm{Idem}_{f}(\mathrm{Sp}) & \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ } & \prod_{p}^{/\mathbf{Q}}\mathrm{Idem}_{f}(\mathrm{Sp}_{(p)})\\
\downarrow & & \downarrow\\
\mathrm{Idem}(\mathrm{Sp}) & \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ } & \prod_{p}^{/\mathbf{Q}}\mathrm{Idem}(\mathrm{Sp}_{(p)})
\end{array}$$
2. For each prime $p$, the projection maps induce a frame embedding
$$\mathrm{Idem}(\mathrm{Sp}_{(p)})\hookrightarrow\prod_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathrm{Idem}(\mathrm{Sp}_{T(n)}).$$
Consequently, there is a sequence of frame embeddings
$$\mathrm{Idem}(\mathrm{Sp})\hookrightarrow\prod_{p}^{/\mathbf{Q}}\prod_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathrm{Idem}(\mathrm{Sp}_{T(n)})\hookrightarrow\prod_{p}\prod_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathrm{Idem}(\mathrm{Sp}_{T(n)}).$$

**Corollary 1.10 (Corollary 6.9).** Let $p$ be a prime. The following diagram of frames commutes, with vertical maps equivalences:
$$\begin{array}{ccc}
\mathrm{Idem}_{f}(\mathrm{Sp}_{(p)}) & \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ } & \varprojlim_{n}\mathrm{Idem}_{f}(L_{n}\mathrm{Sp})\\
\downarrow & & \downarrow\\
\mathrm{Idem}(\mathrm{Sp}_{(p)}) & \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ } & \varprojlim_{n}\mathrm{Idem}(L_{n}\mathrm{Sp})
\end{array}$$
In particular, the telescope inclusion $\mathrm{Idem}_{f}(\mathrm{Sp}_{(p)})\hookrightarrow\mathrm{Idem}(\mathrm{Sp}_{(p)})$ admits a natural frame retraction over $\mathrm{Idem}(\mathrm{Sp}_{\mathbf{Q}})$. This implies that the global telescope inclusion $\mathrm{Idem}_{f}(\mathrm{Sp})\hookrightarrow\mathrm{Idem}(\mathrm{Sp})$ admits a natural frame retraction.

**Theorem F (Corollary 6.15).** The smashing frame $\mathrm{Idem}(\mathrm{Sp})$ is spatial if and only if, for each prime $p$ and each height $n\geq 0$, the smashing frame $\mathrm{Idem}(\mathrm{Sp}_{T(n)})$ is spatial.

## Smashing fields

**Definition 1.11.** Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a symmetric monoidal $\infty$-category whose underlying $\infty$-category has an initial object, and assume the tensor product preserves the initial object in each variable. $\mathcal{C}$ is a *smashing field* if
$$\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{C})=\{\emptyset,\mathbf{1}\}.$$
Equivalently, a smashing field has no nontrivial smashing ideals.

The source records the following examples of smashing fields (see Example 4.53):

| Smashing field | Input / reference |
|---|---|
| $\mathcal{D}(k)$, where $k$ is a field | Neeman's telescope theorem [46] |
| $\mathcal{D}(\mathbf{Z})_{p\text{-}\mathrm{cpl}}$ | detected by the conservative functor $-\otimes^{L}\mathbf{F}_{p}$ |
| $\mathcal{S}$ and $\mathcal{S}_{\leq n}$ for $n\geq -1$ | Theorem 4.54 |
| $\mathcal{S}_{*}$ | Example 4.58 |
| $\mathrm{CMon}(\mathcal{S})$ | Example 4.60 |
| $\mathrm{Sp}_{\geq 0}$ | Example 7.25 |
| $\mathrm{Sp}_{K(n)}$ | [30, Theorem 7.5] |
| $\mathrm{Sp}_{\mathbf{F}_{p}}$ | [57, Proposition 6.2] |
| $\mathrm{Sp}_{I_{p}}$ | [57, Proposition 6.6] |

**Theorem G (Theorem 4.54).** Let $\mathcal{X}\in\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{L})$ be presentably Cartesian symmetric monoidal and nontrivial. Then
$$\mathrm{cIdem}_{f}(\mathcal{X})=\{\emptyset_{\mathcal{X}},*\}.$$
Consequently, the following are equivalent:
1. $\mathcal{X}$ satisfies the telescope conjecture.
2. $\mathcal{X}$ is a smashing field.
3. $\mathcal{X}_{\leq -1}$ consists of only two elements.

## Additive and prestable settings

For a presentably symmetric monoidal, left-complete prestable $\infty$-category $\mathcal{C}$, the smashing frame is identified with the frame of idempotent ideals of $\pi_{0}\mathbf{1}_{\mathcal{C}}$ [37, Theorem D], generalising [26]:
$$\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{C})\simeq\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathrm{Idl}(\mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit})).$$

**Theorem H (Theorem 7.14).** Let $\mathcal{C}\in\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{L}_{\mathrm{ad}})$ be a projectively rigid $\mathrm{Sp}_{\geq 0}$-algebra. Then the following diagram commutes, and its vertical maps are equivalences:
$$\begin{array}{ccc}
\mathrm{cIdem}_{f}(\mathcal{C}) & \hookrightarrow & \mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{C})\\
\downarrow{\scriptstyle\sim} & & \downarrow{\scriptstyle\sim}\\
\mathrm{cIdem}_{f}(\mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit}) & \hookrightarrow & \mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit})\\
\downarrow{\scriptstyle\sim} & & \downarrow{\scriptstyle\sim}\\
\mathrm{Idl}_{p}(\mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit}) & \xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{Im}\ } & \mathrm{cIdem}(\mathrm{Idl}(\mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit}))
\end{array}$$
Here $\mathrm{Idl}_{p}(\mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit})$ denotes the poset of Pierce ideals of $\pi_{0}\mathbf{1}$. In particular, the telescope conjecture holds for $\mathcal{C}$ if and only if it holds for its heart $\mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit}$.

**Serre smashing frame.** For a presentably symmetric monoidal separated Grothendieck prestable $\infty$-category $\mathcal{C}$, the *Serre smashing frame* is
$$\mathrm{cIdem}_{s}(\mathcal{C})\subset\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{C}),$$
consisting of the Serre smashing ideals. It is a subframe (Theorem 8.25).

**Theorem I (Corollary 8.41).** Let $\mathcal{C}\in\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{P}\mathrm{r}^{L}_{\mathrm{ad}})$ be a projectively rigid $\mathrm{Sp}_{\geq 0}$-algebra. Then $\mathrm{cIdem}_{s}(\mathcal{C})$ is spatial, and $\mathrm{Spec}(\mathrm{cIdem}_{s}(\mathcal{C}))$ is quasi-compact.

The *flat telescope conjecture* for $\mathcal{C}$ asks whether the canonical inclusion $\mathrm{cIdem}_{s}(\mathcal{C})\hookrightarrow\mathrm{cIdem}(\mathcal{C})$ is an isomorphism.

**Theorem J (Theorems 7.20, 8.42).** Let $R$ be a connective $\mathbf{E}_{\infty}$-ring. Then every atomic smashing ideal of $\operatorname{Mod}_{R}^{\mathrm{cn}}=\operatorname{Mod}_{R}(\mathrm{Sp}_{\geq 0})$ is a Serre smashing ideal. Hence there is an inclusion of frames
$$\mathrm{cIdem}_{s}(\operatorname{Mod}_{R}^{\mathrm{cn}})\subset\mathrm{cIdem}_{f}(\operatorname{Mod}_{R}^{\mathrm{cn}})\subset\mathrm{cIdem}(\operatorname{Mod}_{R}^{\mathrm{cn}}).$$
This hierarchy corresponds to the following inclusions of idempotent ideals of $\pi_{0}R$:
$$\{\text{Pierce ideals}\}\subset\{\text{pure ideals}\}\subset\{\text{idempotent ideals}\}.$$
For an idempotent ideal $I\subset\pi_{0}R$, the $\infty$-category $\operatorname{aMod}_{(R,I)}(\mathrm{Sp}_{\geq 0})$ of connective almost modules is compact projectively generated if and only if $I$ is a Pierce ideal.
