## Abstract (restated)

The paper formulates differential cohomology and Chern-Weil theory, meaning the theory of connections on fiber bundles and of gauge fields, abstractly in a class of higher toposes called "cohesive". Cocycles in this differential cohomology classify higher principal bundles equipped with cohesive structure (topological, smooth, synthetic differential, supergeometric, etc.) and equipped with connections, hence higher gauge fields.

The paper shows that the cohesive and differential refinement of universal characteristic cocycles is a higher Chern-Weil homomorphism, refined from secondary characteristic classes to morphisms of higher moduli stacks of higher gauge fields. The same refinement constitutes extended geometric prequantization, in the sense of extended or multi-tiered quantum field theory, of hierarchies of higher-dimensional Chern-Simons-type field theories, their higher Wess-Zumino-Witten-type boundary field theories, and all further higher-codimension defect field theories.
