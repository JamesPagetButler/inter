## Abstract and introduction (mathematical content)

- A generalization of the quantum Hall effect is constructed in which particles move in an eight-dimensional space under an SO(8) gauge field.
- The underlying mathematics is that of the octonions, the last normed division algebra.
- Two liquids with distinct configuration spaces are constructed, depending on whether the particles carry spinor or vector SO(8) quantum numbers.
- One liquid lives on a 20-dimensional manifold with an internal component of SO(7) holonomy.
- The second liquid lives on a 14-dimensional manifold with an internal component of G2 holonomy.
