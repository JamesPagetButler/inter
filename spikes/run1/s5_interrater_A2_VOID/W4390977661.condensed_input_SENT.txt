## Paper text

Abstract Faithfully flat descent of pseudo-coherent complexes in rigid geometry was proved by Mathew. In this paper, we generalize the result of Mathew to solid quasi-coherent complexes on rigid analytic varieties, which have been introduced by Clausen and Scholze by means of condensed mathematics.

Introduction We begin with the statement of faithfully flat descent in commutative algebra. Let A -> B be a faithfully flat map of (commutative unital) rings. Faithfully flat descent states that an A-module can be described as a B-module with a descent datum. Recently Lurie and Mathew have generalized the theory of faithfully flat descent to complexes of modules in [Lur18] and [Mat16] by using higher algebra. On the other hand, there is an analogue of the classical theory of faithfully flat descent in rigid geometry. This result has been generalized by Mathew in [Mat22] as follows: Theorem 0.1 ([Mat22, Theorem 1.4]). Let K and A->B be as above. Then we have an equivalence of infinity-categories PCoh(A) -> lim_[n] PCoh(B^{n/A}), where PCoh(A) is the category of pseudo-coherent complexes of A-modules. Recently Clausen and Scholze have developed a new approach to treat such objects in [Sch19, Sch20], which is called condensed mathematics. Theorem 0.2 ([And21, Theorem 4.1]). Let X be an analytic affinoid adic space and let U denote an arbitrary open affinoid subspace of X. Then the functor U -> D((O_X(U), O_X^+(U))_solid) defines a sheaf of infinity-categories on X. In [Mat22], it is expected that faithfully flat descent in rigid geometry can be formulated in this framework. In this paper, we will carry it out. The following is the main theorem in this paper. Theorem 0.3. Let K be a complete non-archimedean field, and let A->B be a faithfully flat map of affinoid K-algebras. Then we have an equivalence of infinity-categories D((A,A^o)_solid) -> lim D((B^{n/A},(B^{n/A})^o)_solid). This theorem is a generalization of Theorem 0.1. We explain two difficulties. First, we briefly review a "descendable property" introduced by Mathew in [Mat16]. In [Mat16, Definition 3.18], Mathew shows that finitely presented faithfully flat maps of discrete rings are descendable ([Mat16, Corollary 3.33]). He also shows that for a descendable map f:A->B of discrete rings, there is an equivalence of infinity-categories D(A) -> lim D(B^{n/A}). Theorem 0.4. There exists an object N_{B/A} in D(B_underline) satisfying: the object is A_solid-complete and compact; we have an equivalence of functors -⊗_{A_solid}^L B_solid -> R Hom_A(N_{B/A},-). By this theorem, we can make use of N_{B/A} instead of B_underline to prove faithfully flat descent. Theorem 0.5. Let A->B be a finitely presented faithfully flat map of discrete rings. Then we have an equivalence of infinity-categories D(A_solid) -> lim D((B^{n/A})_solid). Remark 0.6. While writing this paper, the author was informed that Mann proves faithfully flat descent for discrete rings by a slightly different approach in his thesis [Man22]. In this paper, it will be proved by using N_{B/A}. On the other hand, Mann considers a stable monoidal infinity-category E(A_solid) instead of D(A_solid). By using it instead of N_{B/A}, Mann defines the notion of descendable maps of analytic rings and constructs a general theory of them to prove faithfully flat descent. Outline: Section 1 defines analytic rings, recalling the construction from affinoid pairs ([Sch19, Sch20, And21]). Section 2 proves fppf descent for discrete rings in the context of condensed mathematics. Section 3 introduces adic completeness and small adic rings. Section 4 proves Theorem 0.3. Section 5 recovers Theorem 0.1 from Theorem 0.3, using the method of [And21, Section 5]. Definition 1.1 ([Sch19, Definition 1.2]). A condensed set is a sheaf of sets on the pro-étale site of the point. Definition 1.6 ([Sch19, Definitions 7.1, 7.4]). An uncompleted pre-analytic ring (A,M) is a condensed ring A equipped with a functor {extremally disconnected sets} -> Mod^cond_A, S -> M[S], and a natural transformation. Definition 1.9. For an uncompleted analytic ring (A,M), we define D(A,M) subset D(A) to be the full subcategory of (A,M)-complete complexes. Theorem 1.5 ([Sch19, Theorem 1.10]). The category Mod^cond_A is an abelian category satisfying Grothendieck's axioms (AB3),(AB4),(AB5),(AB6),(AB3*),(AB4*), and is generated by compact projective objects. Construction 1.20 ([Sch19, Example 7.3, Theorem 8.1]). Let A be a finitely generated Z-algebra. We define an uncompleted pre-analytic ring A_solid. Proposition 1.26 ([And21, Proposition 3.34]). Let cAff denote the category of complete affinoid pairs. Then the functor cAff -> AnRing, (A,A^+) -> (A,A^+)_solid is fully faithful, and its image is contained in the full subcategory of 0-truncated analytic rings. Lemma 1.28 ([And21, Lemma 3.8]). The natural map Z_solid -> Z[T]_solid in AnRing is steady. Definition 1.17. A map f:(A,M_A)->(B,M_B) in AnRing is steady if for all maps g:(A,M_A)->(C,M_C) and all M in D(C,M_C), the object M ⊗^L_{(A,M_A)} (B,M_B) is (C,M_C)-complete.
