# Restatement: Gros Topoi as Partially Lax Limits of Petit Topoi

**Note:** The supplied text contains only the title and abstract. It has no introduction and no labelled statements (Theorem, Definition, Proposition, Lemma or Conjecture), so no labelled items are restated below.

## Abstract (restated)

1. The sheaf $\infty$-topos associated to a geometric site in the sense of Lurie can be written as a partially lax limit of smaller sheaf $\infty$-topoi.
2. As a consequence, the $\infty$-topos of $\kappa$-small condensed anima is realized as a partially lax limit of sheaf $\infty$-topoi over extremally disconnected spaces, marked at the open embeddings.
3. Further, a gros version of the étale exodromy theorem is deduced.
