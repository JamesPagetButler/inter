> **Status:** concept first-draft — runs **after Spike 1**, informed by its results. Sequence: 0 lower → 1 active → **2 dream** → 3 ToM. · **Origin:** the second bounded-generativity process the beekeeper named — unresolved material worked by the unconscious offline (vs Spike 1's real-time forward simulation).

## The question
**Can an engineered offline pass over the hypergraph consolidate and refine — turning validated skills into wisdoms and regularising the graph — WITHOUT corrupting settled structure or injecting confident-falsehood?**

## What it grounds in (least-contested science first)
- **Complementary Learning Systems (CLS)** — fast/slow separation + *interleaved* replay avoids catastrophic overwrite.
- **Synaptic Homeostasis (SHY)** — sleep as *competitive* downscaling (weak pruned preferentially), so growth is net of a scheduled subtraction.
- **Overfitted-Brain Hypothesis (Hoel)** — dreams as anti-overfitting *regularisation* (contested; treat as a generative hypothesis, not settled).
- **The federation's own maturation ladder** (`wisdoms/_maturation.md`): Experience → Skill → Wisdom, with promotion triggers (Skill→Wisdom when it recurs across ≥2 tools) + a notary gate + bidirectional demotion. **This is the offline registry-maturation process — Spike 2 lowers/tests it.**

## Candidate assumptions to test (names only)
- **A-interleave:** does *interleaved* F01 (episodic→semantic across many episodes) avoid the catastrophic-overwrite that *batch* F01 causes? (a correction to BMA's existing sleep cycle)
- **A-competitive-decay:** does *competitive* decay (weak/unconsolidated atoms downscale preferentially) preserve consolidated skills better than a flat Ebbinghaus curve?
- **A-promote:** do the Skill→Wisdom promotion triggers fire correctly — promoting genuinely recurrent, cross-tool skills into wisdoms — *without over-promoting* (a rare skill is not a wisdom)? (ties to the "rare ≠ false / rare ≠ general" distinction from Spike 1)
- **A-dreampass:** does a Hoel-style regularisation pass (perturb low-confidence, high-co-occurrence patterns in the CTH `open` quarantine, promote only survivors) improve anything on a *discrete* hypergraph — or is it a category error? (the risky one)

## The safety invariant (inherited from Spike 1)
Dream-generativity happens in the `open` quarantine and only a **reconciled residue** crosses to `settled` — the same separation-of-generation-from-commitment that keeps Spike 1 non-laundering. The dream must never write perturbed content back as a first-class fact.
