## Operational notes
- boxrun's default `--mem-mb 4000` makes it panic, because go-re2 reserves a large block of virtual memory. Use `--mem-mb 12000`.
- Locally, `--all` is a superset of CI's scope. Use `--log-opts` to match CI: CI scans every remote branch because of fetch-depth 0.
- CI side finding: `ci.yml` didn't checksum the tarball. Fixed in Nidavellir#112.
